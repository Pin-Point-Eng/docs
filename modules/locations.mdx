Every space across the property has its own page in PIN — retail stores, restaurants, bathrooms, mechanical rooms, common areas, parking decks, and more. Location pages give anyone on the team a single place to access the full operational history, documents, and equipment for any space, regardless of department.

<Info>
  Anyone can view any location page across the property — locations are not department-restricted, making it easy for any team to find context on any space.
</Info>

## Finding a Location

Open the **Locations module** to see every space on the property. Search by name to find a specific location, or browse the full list. Tap any location to open its full page.

You can also filter the location list by **status** to quickly find every space in a given state, for example every location currently marked Needs Attention or Out of Service / Closed.

## Location Status

Every location has a status that shows its current operational state. The status appears as a pill at the top of the location page, directly below the Location tab. New locations default to **Operational**.

| Status | Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operational** | Green | Default state. The location is functioning normally. |
| **Occupied** | Blue | The location is currently in use. |
| **Needs Attention** | Amber | Something at the location requires review or follow-up. |
| **Out of Service / Closed** | Red | The location is not currently usable, or is closed. |
| **Vacant** | Grey | The location is empty or unoccupied. |

**Who can change it.** Any user with access to the location can change its status.

**How to change it.** Open the location page, tap the status pill below the Location tab, and pick the new status from the dropdown.

**Filtering.** Inside the Locations module, filter the list by status to see every location in a given state at a glance.

## What's on a Location Page

| Tab | What It Contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Location** | Key details (building, levels, tags), photos and document attachments, emergency contacts (store manager name, phone, email), and freeform notes any team member can add. |
| **Notes** | Freeform notes any team member can add — useful for logging observations or operational context about that space. |
| **Pins** | Every Pin ever created at that location, across all departments — active and completed. Toggle **Show Completed** to view historical Pins alongside open ones. |
| **Equipment** | Every asset registered at that space, with condition and department visible at a glance. Tap any asset to jump directly into its equipment record. |

<Info>
  **What you see on the Pins tab depends on your role.** Admins and All-Teams Managers see every Pin at the location. Users see Pins from their department(s) plus any they are following. Some Pins may not be visible if they sit outside your role's scope.
</Info>

## Creating a Pin from a Location

Tap the **\+ Pin** button at the top right of any location page. The Pin automatically inherits that location — no manual setup needed.

## Uploading Documents to a Location

Upload SOPs, floor plans, certificates of insurance, emergency procedures, or any other file directly to a location page. When uploading, choose the access level:

- **Public** — accessible to anyone on the platform
- **Specific access** — restricted to selected individual users and/or entire departments

<Tip>
  Sensitive or location-specific documents — like COIs or emergency contacts — stay exactly where they are needed, accessible to the right people at any time.
</Tip>

Documents stay attached to the location. Anyone with access can also find them in the [Documents module](/modules/documents), which surfaces every document you have permission to view.

## Exporting Locations

The full Locations list view can be exported to a spreadsheet using the **Export** button at the top right. The export includes all relevant fields for every location in the current view.
