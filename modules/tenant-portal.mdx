title: Tenant Portal
description: Let anyone on the property submit a request — no account needed.
---

The Tenant Portal is a dedicated web link that lets tenants, contractors, or anyone else on the property submit a request directly into PIN — without needing an account. Every submission becomes a Pin in the system, routed to the right department, and tracked through to completion alongside everything else.

## How It Works

Anyone can submit a request by visiting your venue's PIN portal link or scanning the QR code. Both can be found on the **home screen of the PIN web app**, below the create button — making them easy to share with tenants at any time.

No account or login is required. The person submitting fills out a short form — title, description, location, and optional photos — and the request is automatically created as a Pin in the system and routed to the appropriate department for triage and resolution.

<Tip>
  QR codes work well posted in tenant spaces and back hallways. The portal link can be shared via email, signage, the tenant handbook, or any other channel. The link can be reused indefinitely — no need to refresh or rotate it.
</Tip>

## Routing Options

Your venue can configure tenant Pins to route in two ways:

- **Triage first** — Pins go to a single triage department which then routes to the correct team
- **Direct routing** — Pins go directly to the intended department

Either way, every request is logged the moment it is submitted — nothing gets lost.

## Email Updates to the Submitter

After submitting a request, the tenant automatically receives email notifications as the Pin's status changes — keeping them informed from intake through completion without anyone on the team having to chase them down.

## How Tenant Pins Behave

Once submitted, tenant Pins follow the same workflow, status updates, and completion flow as any other Pin in the system. They are tagged so the team can identify them at a glance.

## What Tenants Typically Submit

The portal is primarily used by tenants for maintenance and service requests — broken fixtures, HVAC issues, signage problems, and other support needs. It removes the need for phone calls, texts, or emails to report issues.
