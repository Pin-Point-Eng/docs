The Tenant Portal is a dedicated web link that lets tenants, contractors, or anyone else on the property submit a request directly into PIN — without needing an account. Every submission becomes a Pin in the system, routed to the right department, and tracked through to completion alongside everything else.

## How It Works

Anyone can submit a request by visiting your venue's PIN portal link or scanning a QR code. The portal link is on the **home screen of the PIN web app**, below the create button. QR codes are generated from the **Pin QR Generator** (see below).

No account or login is required. The person submitting fills out a short form — title, description, optional photos, and a location if the QR code does not already specify one — and the request is automatically created as a Pin and routed to the team encoded in the QR code.

<Tip>
  QR codes work well posted in tenant spaces and back hallways. The portal link can be shared via email, signage, the tenant handbook, or any other channel. Both the link and QR codes can be reused indefinitely — no need to refresh or rotate them.
</Tip>

## Routing Options

Your venue can configure tenant Pins to route in two ways:

- **Triage first** — Pins go to a single triage department which then routes to the correct team
- **Direct routing** — Pins go directly to the intended department

Either way, every request is logged the moment it is submitted — nothing gets lost.

Admins configure Tenant Portal routing from the venue's global settings.

## Pin QR Generator

Use the Pin QR Generator to create signed QR codes that route submissions to a specific team — for example, the front desk, housekeeping, or maintenance — with an optional pre-set location.

Open it in two places:

- **Create menu in the sidebar** → **Pin QR Generator** for any team and (optionally) any location.
- **A location's page** in the Locations module → **QR Code** button, which pre-fills that location.

To generate a QR code:

1. Select the **Team** that should receive submissions from this QR code.
2. Optionally select a **Location**. If set, the location is locked into the submission and the submitter cannot change it.
3. Select **Generate**.
4. **Copy the link** to share digitally, or **Download PNG** to print a poster that includes the location name, team, and venue.

<Tip>
  Generate a separate QR code for each printed location — for example, one per guest room, conference room, or restroom — and select the team that owns that space. The submitter never has to choose, so requests always land with the right team.
</Tip>

Every QR code is cryptographically signed. If the team or location encoded in the link is changed or removed later, the QR code stops working and the submitter is asked to scan an updated code — regenerate it from the Pin QR Generator to replace it.

## Email Updates to the Submitter

After submitting a request, the tenant automatically receives email notifications as the Pin's status changes — keeping them informed from intake through completion without anyone on the team having to chase them down.

## How Tenant Pins Behave

Once submitted, tenant Pins follow the same workflow, status updates, and completion flow as any other Pin in the system. They are tagged so the team can identify them at a glance.

## What Tenants Typically Submit

The portal is primarily used by tenants for maintenance and service requests — broken fixtures, HVAC issues, signage problems, and other support needs. It removes the need for phone calls, texts, or emails to report issues.
