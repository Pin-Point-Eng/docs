Analytics turns every Pin, incident, asset, and inventory record into operational data — surfacing how your team is performing, which assets need attention, and where issues are clustering across the property.

<Info>
  Analytics is available to **Managers** and **Admins** only. You see analytics for your own department(s) — filtered to the teams you are assigned to. Admins and managers with All-Teams access see the full property.
</Info>

## What Analytics Covers

### Operational Performance — Pins & Incidents

- Completion and clearance rates
- Response and resolution times
- Workload by team and user
- Overdue and urgent counts
- Created-vs-completed trends over time
- Department performance cards for side-by-side comparison

<Info>
  Single-instance Pins and Recurring Pin instances are tracked separately — useful for distinguishing day-to-day reactive work from scheduled routines like inspections and preventive maintenance. Incidents have their own dashboard for response and on-scene metrics.
</Info>

### Asset & Inventory Health — Equipment & Inventory

- Fleet health and condition breakdown across every asset
- Average age and assets approaching end of life
- Breakdowns by type and department
- Total inventory items and value by category and supplier
- Stock items below threshold — see the full picture of what you own and what you are running low on

### AI-Powered Insights

- **Ask Analytics** any question in plain language and get an instant answer — no report-building required
- **AI-generated health reports** for any asset or location — summarize recurring issues, maintenance frequency, and performance trends in one click
- **Hotspot detection** automatically surfaces areas of the property where Pins are clustering above normal

## Navigating Analytics

1. Open **Analytics** from the left navigation
2. Switch between **Pins, Incidents, Equipment,** and **Inventory** dashboards
3. Use the **date range picker** to set the time window (last 7, 30, 90 days, or custom)
4. Filter by **team** to focus on a specific department or group
5. Hover or tap any chart for detailed breakdowns
6. Use the **team filter** to compare departments side-by-side
7. **Ask a question** in plain language to get an instant answer
