Documents live throughout PIN — uploaded directly to the Equipment, Locations, Pins, Incidents, and Projects where the work happens. The **Documents module** brings them all into one place so you can find any document you have access to without navigating into individual records.

<Info>
  Documents stay attached to their source — an SOP uploaded to a piece of equipment still lives on that equipment page. The Documents module is an access layer, not separate storage.
</Info>

## What You'll See in the Module

The Documents module surfaces every document you have permission to view:

- Documents on **Pins, Incidents, and Projects** you follow
- Documents on **Equipment and Locations** that are marked Public, or that you've been given specific access to

Everything is searchable and filterable so you can quickly find what you need — manuals, SOPs, COIs, floor plans, incident reports, and more.

## Where to Upload Documents

Documents can be uploaded from any of the following:

| Source | Where the document lives |
| --- | --- |
| **Equipment** | Attached to the asset's record, history, and details. |
| **Locations** | Attached to the space's page alongside all its relevant information. |
| **Pins** | Attached to the Pin's Info tab. |
| **Incidents** | Attached to the incident record and information. |
| **Projects** | Attached to the project's Documents area, alongside files surfaced from linked Pins. |

## How Document Visibility Works

Document visibility depends on where the document is uploaded.

### Pins, Incidents, and Projects

These are **follower-based** modules. Documents uploaded inside them are visible to anyone following that parent record:

- **Project documents** are visible to project followers.
- **Incident documents** are visible to incident followers.
- **Pin documents** live on the Pin and are accessible to anyone who can view the Pin.

### Equipment and Locations

When uploading a document to a piece of equipment or a location, the uploader chooses one of two access models:

| Access | Who Can See It |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone on the platform. |
| **Specific access** | Only the individual users and/or departments selected by the uploader. |

This lets sensitive files — like COIs, contracts, or restricted SOPs — stay locked down to the right people, while general reference materials remain available to everyone.

## Common Examples

- **Equipment manuals & warranties** — uploaded to the asset, set to Public so any team needing them can pull them up.
- **Tenant COIs** — uploaded to a Location, restricted to the leasing and security departments.
- **Incident reports & supporting photos** — uploaded to the incident, visible to incident followers.
- **Project deliverables** — uploaded to the project, visible to project followers.
- **Pin attachments** — uploaded to a Pin to provide context for the work being done.
