Documents in PIN live with the work they belong to. Upload them to **Equipment, Locations, Pins, Incidents, or Projects** — wherever they're relevant. The central **Documents module** then surfaces every document you have permission to view in one place, so you can find any file without navigating into individual records.

<Info>
  The Documents module is an **access layer**, not separate storage. Files stay attached to their original record — the module just gives you a single place to find them.
</Info>

## Where Documents Live

Documents can be uploaded to any of the following:

- **Equipment** — manuals, warranties, service records, inspection reports.
- **Locations** — SOPs, floor plans, certificates of insurance, emergency procedures, signage standards.
- **Pins** — reference files, photos, supporting documents tied to a specific task.
- **Incidents** — reports, witness statements, photo evidence, supporting documentation.
- **Projects** — scope documents, plans, contracts, deliverables.

Every upload stays with its source record. The Documents module reads from all of them.

## Access & Visibility

Document visibility depends on where the document is uploaded:

| Where uploaded | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Pin** | Live on the Pin, accessible to anyone who can view it. |
| **Project** | Visible to followers of the project. |
| **Incident** | Visible to followers of the incident. |
| **Equipment** | At upload, the uploader chooses **Public** (anyone on the platform) or **Specific access** (selected users and/or departments). |
| **Location** | Same as Equipment — **Public** or **Specific access**. |

The Documents module respects these rules — you only see documents you have permission to view through their source record.

## Using the Documents Module

Open the Documents module to:

- **Browse** every document you have access to across the platform.
- **Search** by name to find a specific file fast.
- **Filter** by source type — Equipment, Locations, Pins, Incidents, or Projects — to narrow the view.
- **Open the source record** directly from any document, so you always have full context.

## Uploading a Document

1. Open the record where the document belongs — an Equipment record, Location page, Pin, Incident, or Project.
2. Tap **Add Document** (or the Documents tab, depending on the record).
3. Select the file from your device.
4. On Equipment and Locations, set the access level — **Public** or **Specific access**.
5. Save.

The document is now attached to that record and surfaces in the central Documents module for anyone with access.

## What to Upload

Good candidates for PIN documents:

- **SOPs and standard work** — attach to the location or equipment the routine applies to.
- **Manuals and warranties** — attach to the equipment record so technicians have them on hand.
- **Certificates of insurance (COIs)** — attach to the location or contractor's tenant record.
- **Floor plans and layouts** — attach to the location for reference.
- **Inspection reports and photos** — attach to the related Pin, equipment, or incident.
- **Project deliverables** — attach to the project for everyone following along.
