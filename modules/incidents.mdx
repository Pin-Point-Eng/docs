The Incidents module is built for tracking and documenting events that require an officer response and a formal record — security, safety, and medical situations across the property. Unlike regular Pins, incidents have their own dedicated workflow including a structured status timeline, formal reporting, and follower-based visibility.

## Incident Types

| Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Security** | Theft, shoplifting, suspicious activity, property damage. |
| **Safety** | Hazards, evacuations, fire alarms, safety-related events. |
| **Medical** | Guest or staff injuries, medical emergencies, health-related incidents. |

The category drives how the incident is routed and reported.

## Who Can See an Incident

Incidents are **follower-based** — only users added as followers can view, interact with, or see the chat for an incident. This keeps sensitive information visible to the right people only.

## Creating an Incident

Tap the **\+** button within the Incidents module and fill in:

- **Title** (required) — what happened
- **Description** — additional details
- **Incident Type** (required) — Security, Safety, or Medical
- **Assignee** — the officer responding
- **Followers** — anyone who needs visibility
- **Location** — where the incident took place

Once created, the incident is placed on the property map and appears in the Incidents module for all followers.

## The Status Timeline

Every incident moves through five timestamped stages:

| Status | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Reported and active. |
| **Dispatched** | An officer has been dispatched to the scene. |
| **On Scene** | The officer has arrived at the location. |
| **Cleared** | Resolved on the ground. |
| **Closed** | Formally closed and documented. |

<Warning>
  Only **Incident Managers** can close an incident. They can move an incident from any active state to closed and have access to view all closed incidents and their reports. Standard officers and followers can update status and file reports but cannot close out an incident.
</Warning>

## Incident Reports

The **Reports tab** allows the assigned officer to file structured reports documenting what happened. Multiple reports can be added to a single incident — each capturing who filed it, when, and the full event details. Reports are stored permanently as part of the incident record.

## Incident Chat

Every incident has its own chat accessible to all followers — used for real-time communication throughout the response. Use regular messages for conversation, or post a formal **Update** (highlighted in green) to flag important developments that all followers need to see.

## Incident Documents

Documents can be uploaded directly to an incident — supporting photos, statements, reports, or any other file related to the event. Anyone following the incident can view its documents, and they're also surfaced in the central [Documents module](/modules/documents) for quick access.

## Converting a Pin to an Incident

Any regular Pin can be converted to an incident from the three-dot (⋯) menu. This is useful when a task also requires a formal record — for example, a maintenance Pin for graffiti removal that also needs a security incident logged alongside it. The work continues as a Pin while the incident is separately documented through the Incidents module.
