Vendors is where you keep track of the companies and people your venue works with — suppliers, contractors, service providers, and anyone else you buy from or call in for a job. Each vendor gets its own profile with contact details and a list of the inventory items sourced from them.

## Where to find it

Vendors is a top-level module in the sidebar, alongside Equipment, Inventory, and Documents.

## Access

Vendors are scoped by department. You can see and edit vendors that belong to your department(s). Admins and All-Teams Managers see every vendor across the venue.

Documents attached to a vendor have their own permission layer on top of that. See [Documents](#documents) below.

## What's on a vendor profile

Each vendor profile has five sections:

### Info

The basics about the vendor:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Company or vendor name |
| **Type** | What kind of vendor — supplier, contractor, service provider, etc. |
| **Description** | Notes about what they do, terms, or anything else worth remembering |

### Contact list

A vendor can have multiple contacts — useful when you deal with different people for sales, service, or accounts. Each contact has:

- Full name
- Role
- Email
- Phone

Mark one contact as **Primary** so your team knows who to call first.

### Linked inventory

Every inventory item sourced from this vendor shows up here automatically. Click any item to jump to its inventory page.

### Linked equipment

Every piece of equipment linked to this vendor shows up here automatically. Click any item to jump to its equipment page.

### Documents

Upload files directly to a vendor profile. Good candidates:

- Contracts and service agreements
- W9s and tax forms
- Certificates of insurance
- Price lists and quotes
- Product catalogs or spec sheets

When uploading, choose the access level:

- **Public** accessible to anyone who can view the vendor
- **Specific access** restricted to selected users and/or departments

Vendor documents also surface in the [Documents module](/modules/documents) for anyone with permission to view them.

## Creating a vendor

1. Open **Vendors** from the sidebar.
2. Tap **New Vendor**.
3. Fill in the Info fields, then add one or more contacts.
4. Save.

You can link inventory items to the vendor from the inventory item itself (see below) — you don't have to do it from the vendor page.

## Linking a vendor to an inventory item or equipment

Vendors can be linked to both inventory items and equipment.

**Inventory:** on any inventory item, the **Vendor** field lets you pick a vendor from the list. Once linked, the inventory item shows the vendor's name and the vendor profile shows the item under **Linked Inventory**. Each inventory item links to one vendor.

**Equipment:** on any equipment record, the **Vendor** field lets you pick a vendor from the list. Once linked, the equipment record shows the vendor and the vendor profile shows the asset under **Linked Equipment**. Each piece of equipment links to one vendor.

A vendor can be linked to as many inventory items and equipment records as you need. Available on both web and mobile.

## Managing vendors

- **Edit** any field on a vendor profile at any time.
- **Delete** a vendor when you no longer work with them. Any inventory items or equipment linked to that vendor will have their Vendor field cleared — the items themselves aren't affected.
- **Export** the full Vendors list view to a spreadsheet using the **Export** button at the top right. The export includes all relevant fields for every vendor in the current view.

## When to use it

- You want one source of truth for who supplies what
- You're tired of retyping the same supplier name across dozens of inventory items
- You need contact info handy when something breaks or runs out
- You want to see everything you buy from a given vendor in one view
