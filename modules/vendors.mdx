Vendors is where you keep track of the companies and people your venue works with — suppliers, contractors, service providers, and anyone else you buy from or call in for a job. Each vendor gets its own profile with contact details and a list of the inventory items sourced from them.

## Where to find it

Vendors is a top-level module in the sidebar, alongside Equipment, Inventory, and Documents.

## What's on a vendor profile

Each vendor profile has three sections:

### Info

The basics about the vendor:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Company or vendor name |
| **Type** | What kind of vendor — supplier, contractor, service provider, etc. |
| **Description** | Notes about what they do, terms, or anything else worth remembering |

### Contact list

A vendor can have multiple contacts — useful when you deal with different people for sales, service, or accounts. Each contact has:

- Full name
- Role
- Email
- Phone

Mark one contact as **Primary** so your team knows who to call first.

### Linked inventory

Every inventory item sourced from this vendor shows up here automatically. Click any item to jump to its inventory page.

## Creating a vendor

1. Open **Vendors** from the sidebar.
2. Tap **New Vendor**.
3. Fill in the Info fields, then add one or more contacts.
4. Save.

You can link inventory items to the vendor from the inventory item itself (see below) — you don't have to do it from the vendor page.

## Linking a vendor to an inventory item

On any inventory item, the **Vendor** field lets you pick a vendor from the list. Once linked:

- The inventory item shows the vendor's name (tap to open the profile)
- The vendor profile shows the item under **Linked Inventory**

Each inventory item links to one vendor. A vendor can be linked to as many items as you need.

## Managing vendors

- **Edit** any field on a vendor profile at any time.
- **Delete** a vendor when you no longer work with them. Any inventory items linked to that vendor will have their Vendor field cleared — the items themselves aren't affected.

## When to use it

- You want one source of truth for who supplies what
- You're tired of retyping the same supplier name across dozens of inventory items
- You need contact info handy when something breaks or runs out
- You want to see everything you buy from a given vendor in one view
