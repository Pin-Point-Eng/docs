Pinny is your workspace assistant — an AI agent built into PIN that helps you with anything PIN-related. Ask questions, get data, analyze trends, trigger changes, or get a hand with your work.

Pinny is available on the **web app only**. It's turned on per venue by the PIN team. If you don't see Pinny in your top bar, reach out to your account manager.

## Where to find Pinny

Pinny sits in the **top bar** on every page of the web app. Click to open it in a **side panel** that stays open as you move around.

Ask by typing a message, or attach **photos** and **PDFs** to give Pinny more context.

## What Pinny can do

Pinny is a full AI agent that can touch anything PIN-related. In short, if PIN can do it, Pinny can help you with it.

### Ask about anything

- Pull up information on Pins, incidents, equipment, inventory, locations, vendors, procedures, projects, documents, and chats
- Analyze trends, surface patterns, and summarize activity
- Answer questions on how PIN works or how to do something
- Search and read chats you're a member of, including incident case numbers and notes, Pin resolution notes, activity logs, and documents you can open

### Create records

Pinny can prepare any of the following for you to approve:

- Pins, recurring Pin series, incidents, projects, and procedures
- Equipment and inventory items
- A project together with its Pins in one go
- A new Pin with a checklist or procedure already attached

### Make changes

Pinny can also prepare changes to existing records:

- Add notes to equipment, inventory, and locations
- Attach a procedure to a Pin
- Add files you send Pinny to a Pin
- Edit a procedure in the library
- Resolve, cancel, reassign, or change the status of many Pins at once from a single review card

## How Pinny works — review cards

Pinny never makes changes directly. When you ask Pinny to do something, it prepares the change and shows it to you on a **review card**.

- **Review** the proposed change — Pinny shows every detail, including attached files as thumbnails.
- **Approve** the card and Pinny applies the change.
- **Modify the request** (for example, change the assignee or add a detail) and the review card replaces itself with the updated version.
- **Dismiss** the card and nothing happens.

For steps where Pinny needs more information, it asks for everything missing in **one card with suggested answers** rather than one question at a time.

### Bulk actions on review cards

For bulk changes (resolve, cancel, reassign, change status on many Pins), the review card shows every selected Pin with a **checkbox**. Uncheck any Pin you want to leave out. If a Pin can't be changed — for example, because of permissions or its current state — the card shows the **reason** next to it.

## What Pinny can see

Pinny only sees what your role gives you access to. It doesn't bypass permissions. If you can't see something in PIN, Pinny can't see it either.

This means Pinny's answers reflect your personal scope — your departments, your Pins, your chats, and the records your role can read.

## Tips for working with Pinny

- **Talk naturally.** Ask the way you'd ask a colleague.
- **Attach context** when a photo or document would help — Pinny reads both.
- **Ask follow-up questions** to narrow or expand the result.
- **Trust the review step.** Pinny won't change anything until you approve it, so experiment freely with how you phrase your requests.
