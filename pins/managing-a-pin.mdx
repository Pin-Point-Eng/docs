Opening a Pin gives you everything you need to act on it, communicate about it, and track it through to completion. Tap any Pin in your list or on the map to open it. Inside you will find two tabs: **Info** and **Chat**.

## The Info Tab

The Info tab is where you take action on a Pin. From here you can update the status, view the location and get directions, check off checklist items, add photos or attachments, add or edit followers, and link equipment or inventory. Managers can also reassign a Pin to a different user or department directly from the Info tab.

Pins scheduled with a **Start Date** show a **Scheduled** badge on the Pin card and inside the Info tab, displaying the date and time work is set to begin.

Documents and files uploaded to a Pin live on the Pin and are accessible to anyone who can view it. They're also surfaced in the central [Documents module](/modules/documents) for quick reference.

## Followers

A Follower is anyone who has visibility into a Pin and receives notifications when it is updated.

The following users are automatically added as followers when a Pin is created:

- **Creator** — The user who created the Pin.
- **Assignee** — The user or department the Pin is assigned to.
- **Reassigner** — Anyone who reassigns a Pin to another user automatically becomes a follower.

Additional followers can be added at any time — either when creating the Pin, from the Info tab, or by @ mentioning a user in the Pin's chat.

## Pin Actions

From the three-dot (⋯) menu at the top right of any Pin, a manager can:

- **Mark as Priority** — flag the Pin so the assignee knows to treat it urgently. Priority Pins are visually distinct in the list and on the map, and can be filtered to surface the most important work first.
- **Add, edit, or remove a start date** — schedule when the work is meant to begin. Pins with a start date show a **Scheduled** badge with the date and time.
- **Add, edit, or remove a due date** — give the team a clear deadline. Due dates power overdue tracking, the Overdue & Due Soon section on the Overview, and date-based filters.
- **Edit the title and description**
- **Add the Pin to a Group or Project**
- **Clone the Pin** — see [Cloning a Pin](#cloning-a-pin) below.
- **Convert to Incident** — turn the Pin into a formal incident record when a task also requires structured incident documentation. See [Incidents](/modules/incidents) for details.
- **Share or export the Pin**

## Cloning a Pin

Any Pin can be cloned into a new Pin, on both web and mobile. Cloning is useful when you need to log a similar task quickly — for example, repeating a repair at a different location or spinning up variants of the same request across zones.

From the three-dot (⋯) menu on any Pin, tap **Clone Pin**. Every field carries over — title, description, assignee, location, priority, due date, start date, checklist, linked equipment, linked inventory, followers, group, project, category, tags, and attachments. Chat history does not carry over.

A **review screen** opens with everything pre-filled. Adjust any field before submitting — the clone doesn't create a new Pin until you confirm.

Any Pin can be cloned regardless of status. Any user can clone.

## AI Duplicate Pin Detection

PIN's AI checks every new Pin against existing open Pins to catch potential duplicates before they clutter your workload. If a match is found, a **Potential duplicate detected** banner appears on the Pin's Info tab after it's created.

The banner shows:

- A plain-language explanation of why the match was flagged (for example, "Both reports are for the same place with identical titles, reported about 8 hours apart").
- The potential duplicate Pin, with its title and distance from the current Pin's location. Tap it to open the original.

You have two options:

- **Not a duplicate** — dismisses the banner and keeps the Pin active.
- **Cancel duplicate** — cancels the current Pin as a duplicate of the existing one.

Available on both web and mobile.

## Linking Equipment and Inventory After Creation

Equipment and inventory can be linked to a Pin at any time from the Pin's detail page.

- **Equipment** — link one or more equipment items. When multiple are linked, choose which items have their condition updated when the Pin is completed, and which item's location the Pin inherits. The Pin appears in the full history of every linked equipment item.
- **Inventory** — link one or more inventory items and log the quantity used, either at link time or later from the inventory item's detail page. Any usage automatically reduces on-hand quantity in the [Inventory module](/modules/inventory) and is written to the item's history. If the on-hand quantity drops below the minimum threshold, a restock Pin is automatically created.

Any user with permission to edit inventory can log inventory used on a Pin.

## Reassigning a Pin

When a Pin needs to move to a different person or team, managers can reassign it from the Info tab — open the Pin, tap the assignee, and select a new department or user.

- The new assignee is notified immediately and the Pin moves into their feed.
- The previous assignee no longer owns the Pin, but stays as a follower so they keep visibility.
- Whoever reassigns the Pin is also automatically added as a follower.

This keeps everyone who has touched the Pin in the loop without anyone needing to manually re-add followers.

## Pin Status Guide

Keeping the Pin's status up to date lets the creator and your team know exactly where things stand without needing to ask. Once a status is updated, a notification gets triggered to all the Pin's followers.

| Status | When to Use |
| --- | --- |
| To Do | The Pin has been created and is waiting to be started. This is the default status. |
| In Progress | Update to this as soon as you start working. This tells the creator and your manager that work has begun. |
| Blocked | Use this when something is preventing you from completing the task — waiting on a part, access, or another team. Leave a chat message explaining what is blocking you. |
| Cancelled | The task is no longer needed and will not be completed. |

## The Chat Tab

The Chat tab is where all communication about this specific Pin happens. Everyone involved  — the creator, assignee, department managers, and followers — is part of the same conversation.

- Send messages, share photos and files
- **@ mention** users to add them as followers instantly
- View the **activity log** — a complete timestamped record of every action taken on the Pin

### Messages vs. Updates

Within any chat, you have two ways to communicate:

- **Regular message** — standard conversational chat used for questions or back-and-forth discussion
- **Update** — a formal, highlighted message (shown in green) used to post important information that all followers should be aware of

## Bulk Actions (Web Only)

On the web app, you can action multiple Pins at once. From the Pins list or from within a group, use the checkbox next to each Pin — or **Select All** — to pick the Pins you want, then choose an action:

- **Assign to a user**
- **Change status**
- **Add to or remove from a group**
- **Delete** (roles with Pin deletion permission only)

Pins from different departments can be selected in the same bulk action. Any user can use bulk actions, but only for actions their role allows. Standard follower and notification rules apply across every selected Pin — the person taking the action becomes a follower, and assignees receive their usual notifications.

Bulk actions are available on web only, not mobile.

## Completing a Pin

When the work is done, tap **Mark Complete**. You can:

- Add completion notes
- Upload a completion photo
- Log time spent (hours and minutes)
- Update the condition of linked equipment — if multiple equipment items are linked, condition updates apply to the items you selected on the Pin

Once marked complete, all followers are notified and the Pin disappears from active views but remains fully searchable.
