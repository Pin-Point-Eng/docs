---
title: Creating a Pin
description: Creating a Pin is quick and straightforward — it only takes a few seconds to report an issue, request work, or flag anything that needs attention.
---

Anyone can create a Pin at any time — for your own department, another department, or a specific individual. When a Pin is assigned to a department, it goes to that department's managers to route to the right person.

To create a Pin, tap the **+** button. You can fill in the Pin's details manually, or use Snap-to-Pin to let AI do the work for you.

## Snap-to-Pin ✨

Snap-to-Pin is PIN's built-in AI feature that makes creating a Pin even faster. Instead of typing out the details manually, simply tap the AI sparkle icon ✨ at the top of the create screen, take a photo of any issue, and PIN's AI will instantly fill in the title, description, and suggested department for you.

<Warning>
  Always double-check the AI-generated information for accuracy before submitting.
</Warning>

## Manually Input: What to Fill In

- **Title** (required). Be specific — a clear title helps the right team act faster.
- **Description** (optional but recommended). Include any detail that helps the team understand the issue.
- **Assignee** (required). Choose a department or a specific individual.
- **Media** (optional). A photo or video helps the receiving team understand the issue immediately.

## Optional Details

| Field | What It Does |
|-------|-------------|
| Followers | Add users who should have visibility and receive updates. The creator and assignee are added automatically. |
| Due Date | Set a deadline for when the task needs to be completed. |
| Priority | Flag the Pin so the assignee knows to treat it urgently. |
| Link Equipment | Attach this Pin to a specific piece of equipment to build its maintenance history. |
| Checklist | Add step-by-step items that need to be completed as part of the task. |
| Group | Add the Pin to a group — a folder for organizing similar Pins together. |
| Tags | Add one or more tags to help categorize and filter the Pin. |
| Private | Make the Pin visible only to you and the assignee. |

## Groups

Groups are folders that organize similar Pins together. Any user can create a group, and groups are visible across all departments. To create a group, navigate to the Groups section within the Pins module and tap the create button. Only a name is required.

## Private Pins

Toggling a Pin to Private makes it visible only to you and the person it is assigned to — no one else on the platform can see it. Private Pins are ideal for logging personal tasks, reminders, or notes that you need to track for yourself but do not need to share with your team.

## Setting the Location

Every Pin needs a location on the map. Tap **Select Location** and either use the blue dot while on property to place the Pin at your exact current position, search for a space by name, or let it auto-populate if you linked equipment.

If you select a location with registered equipment, PIN will ask if you want to link it and optionally update its condition.

<Info>
  If you see something that needs attention anywhere on the property — drop a Pin. Even if it's not your job to fix it, it is your job to flag it.
</Info>
