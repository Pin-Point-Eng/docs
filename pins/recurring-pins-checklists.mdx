Recurring Pins are how PIN automates the routines that run your venue every day — opening checklists, hourly patrols, weekly inspections, and preventive maintenance. Build a series once and PIN generates a fresh Pin on schedule, every time it's due.

## Series vs. Instance

| Concept | What It Is |
| --- | --- |
| **Series** | The master template that defines the routine — title, checklist items, assignee, location or equipment, and cadence. Build it once; every future instance is generated from it. |
| **Instance** | The individual Pin generated each time the series runs. Appears in the assignee's feed like a regular Pin, marked with the recurring arrows icon. Yesterday's and today's checklists are completely separate Pins. |

<Info>
  Completing or cancelling one instance has no effect on the series or any other instances.
</Info>

## Cadences

`Hourly` `Daily` `Weekly` `Biweekly` `Monthly` `Every 3 Months` `Every 6 Months` `Yearly`

## Examples

- **Daily Bathroom Checklist** — Housekeeping walks every restroom each morning, checking cleanliness, supplies, and equipment.
- **Hourly Security Patrol** — Officers complete a patrol route every hour, logging each zone as they go.
- **Weekly Fire Extinguisher Inspection** — Maintenance verifies pressure, tags, and accessibility across the property.
- **Monthly HVAC Preventive Maintenance** — Engineering services each unit on schedule and logs the work against the asset.
- **Quarterly Wayfinding Audit** — Operations reviews every kiosk and signage location for damage, accuracy, and content updates.

## Creating a Recurring Pin

Who can create a recurring Pin depends on your venue's role configuration. Anyone with permission can create one from the **Recurring Pins module** by tapping the create button, or by tapping **\+ Create a recurring Pin instead** from within the standard Pin creation screen.

**Required fields:**

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Series Title | The name of the recurring routine. |
| Assignee | Choose the department or individual you want receiving the Pin each time it's generated. If a department, a manager will route each instance. |
| Frequency | How often the Pin generates. |
| First Occurrence | The date and time the first instance will be generated. |
| Days to Complete | How many days the assignee has to complete each instance before it becomes overdue. |

**Additional fields:**

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Checklist | Step-by-step items the assignee works through inside each instance. Items can be added or removed at any time. |
| Equipment | Link the series to a specific piece of equipment so every completed instance is logged to that asset's maintenance history. |
| Location | Anchor the series to a specific location on the map. Every generated instance inherits this location. |
| Group | Add the series to a Group to keep related routines organized together. Every future instance inherits the Group automatically. |

## How Recurring Pins Appear in Your Feed

- If assigned to your **department**, it will appear in your Team view.
- If assigned directly to **you**, it will appear in your My Pins feed with a notification.
- **Followers** added to a recurring series carry through to every instance — add someone once and they are looped in on every occurrence going forward.

When a new instance generates, the assignee is notified just like any other Pin assignment. Followers receive notifications on status changes, chat messages, and completion — same as regular Pins.

## How to Work a Recurring Pin

1. **Open the Recurring Pin** from your Pins list — look for the recurring arrows icon.
2. **Work through the checklist items** by tapping each item as you go through your routine. Checklist items can be added or removed at any time from within the Pin.
3. **Found an issue?** Long press/hold on that checklist item to instantly create a new Pin, with the key details already pre-filled and automatically routed to the correct department.
4. **Mark the Recurring Pin complete** when done and add any completion notes or time to complete information.

### Overdue Instances

If a recurring Pin instance is not completed before the next one generates, it becomes overdue and stays in the assignee's feed **alongside** the newly generated instance until it is completed or cancelled. Overdue instances also appear in the Overdue & Due Soon section of the Overview dashboard.

Any instance can be cancelled by anyone — tap cancel and provide a reason.

### What Happens When an Instance Is Completed

When a recurring Pin instance is marked complete:

- All followers are notified.
- The instance moves out of active views but stays fully searchable.
- If the series is linked to a piece of equipment, the completion is logged to that asset's maintenance history.
- The completion contributes to the series' historical record, viewable from the options tab (see _Viewing Previous Instances_ below).
- Recurring Pin completions are tracked separately from one-off Pin completions in Analytics, so you can distinguish reactive work from scheduled routines.

## Managing a Recurring Series

All recurring Pin series can be found in the **Recurring Pins module**. You can also access series options from within any recurring Pin instance by tapping the **purple marker** at the top of the Pin.

From the options tab you can:

- View the full series details and all past, present, and future instances
- **Edit the schedule** — change assignee, cadence, checklist, or any other detail
- **Clone the series** — creates an exact duplicate you can rename and configure independently, useful for setting up a similar routine for another zone or department without rebuilding from scratch
- **Add to or manage a group**
- **Pause the series** — stops all future instances from generating until unpaused. Any currently open instances remain active and must be completed or cancelled individually.
- **End the series** — permanently terminates it

### Series vs. Instance Edits

When making any change to a recurring Pin, you choose whether the change applies to:

- **The full series going forward** — affects every future instance the series generates.
- **That one specific instance** — affects only the open Pin in front of you, leaving the series and all other instances untouched.

This lets you adjust today's checklist for a one-off situation without altering the routine itself, or update the routine permanently when something about the work changes.

## Viewing Previous Instances

From within the options tab, view all past completed instances of any recurring series — a full historical record of every time that routine was completed.
