# Chat and Notifications

Pin keeps communication close to the work. Instead of spreading context across radio calls, texts, and email, task-specific communication stays with the Pin.

For daily chat basics, see [Chat & Communication](/communication/chat).

## Types of chat

**Pin Chat** is the conversation tied to a specific Pin. Followers, the creator, the assignee, and relevant managers may be included automatically.

**Direct Messages** are one-on-one conversations with another user.

**Group Chats** are conversations with multiple users across teams or departments.

**Team Chat** is a department-level conversation that includes team members.

## Messages and updates

A message is a normal chat message for questions, notes, and back-and-forth discussion.

An update is a more formal progress note or important announcement. If your workspace shows updates differently, use them for information that followers should be able to find quickly later.

## Pin Chat best practices

Use Pin Chat for task-specific questions, decisions, photos, videos, files, and status context.

Use @ mentions to bring a teammate into the conversation. In many workflows, @ mentioning someone adds them as a follower and notifies them.

Use the activity log when you need a timestamped record of actions taken on the Pin.

## Notifications

Notifications help you track activity that needs attention. You may receive notifications when:

- A Pin is assigned to you
- A Pin is assigned to your department
- You are added as a follower
- Someone messages a Pin chat you are part of
- A Pin you follow changes status
- Work becomes due or overdue
