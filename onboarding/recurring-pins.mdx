# Recurring Pins and Checklists

Recurring Pins help teams avoid recreating the same task or checklist over and over. They generate automatically on a schedule and appear in the work feed like regular Pins.

For basic creation steps, see [Recurring Pins & Checklists](/pins/recurring-pins-checklists#creating-a-recurring-pin).

## Series and instances

A recurring series is the repeating template. It defines the routine, checklist, assignment, location, and schedule.

An instance is the individual Pin generated each time the series runs. Completing or cancelling one instance does not complete or cancel the whole series.

Example: a daily opening checklist runs every morning. Each day creates a separate checklist instance.

## Cadences

Recurring Pins may run hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, every 3 months, every 6 months, or yearly.

## Where recurring Pins appear

When a recurring Pin instance generates, it appears in the Pins list like a regular Pin. It may be marked with a recurring icon.

If it is assigned to your department, it appears in Team Pins. If it is assigned directly to you, it appears in My Pins and you may receive a notification.

## Work a recurring checklist

1. Open the recurring Pin from your Pins list.
2. Work through checklist items one by one.
3. Mark checklist items complete as you finish them.
4. If you find an issue, create a separate Pin from that checklist item if the app offers that action.
5. Mark the recurring Pin complete when the routine is done.
6. Add completion notes or time spent if required.

If you find an issue during a checklist, do not simply check it off and move on. Create a separate Pin so the issue is tracked and resolved.

## Overdue instances

If a recurring Pin instance is not completed before the next one generates, it can become overdue and remain in the feed until it is completed or cancelled.

If an instance should not be completed, cancel the individual instance and provide a reason. Cancelling one instance does not stop future scheduled instances.

## Managing a recurring series

Recurring series can be managed from the Recurring Pins module or from a recurring Pin instance, depending on your workspace.

Common series actions include:

- View full series details
- View past, present, and future instances
- Edit the schedule
- Clone the series
- Add to or manage a group
- Pause future instances
- End the series

When changing a recurring Pin, pay attention to whether the change applies only to one instance or to the full series.

Pausing a series stops future instances from generating until it is unpaused. Any currently open instances remain active and should still be completed or cancelled individually.
