# Onboarding Guide

Use this guide to train new users, refresh your team, or understand how Pin fits into daily operations across a property.

Pin is built around a simple idea: if something needs attention, create a Pin. The Pin is tied to the right location, assigned to the right person or department, and tracked until the work is complete.

## What Pin manages

Pin can be used for:

- Day-to-day tasks and work orders
- Recurring inspections, patrols, checklists, and preventive maintenance
- Equipment and asset tracking
- Inventory and parts management
- Vendors, contractors, and suppliers — with contacts and linked inventory
- Team communication inside tasks, teams, groups, and direct messages
- Multi-department projects and initiatives
- Security, safety, and medical incidents
- Tenant or external requests through a portal or QR code

## Roles during onboarding

Managers usually learn team visibility, reassignment, priorities, projects, analytics, equipment, and inventory management.

Users usually learn how to create Pins, find assigned work, update status, use chat, complete tasks, and update equipment condition when needed.

Your exact access depends on your organization and permissions.
