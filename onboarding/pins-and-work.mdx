# Pins and Daily Work

A Pin is the basic unit of work in Pin. It can be a task, issue, request, work order, or reminder that needs to be assigned, tracked, discussed, and completed.

For short task-based instructions, see [Creating a Pin](/pins/creating-a-pin) and [Managing a Pin](/pins/managing-a-pin).

## Life of a Pin

1. Something needs attention on the property.
2. A Pin is created and assigned to a department, person, or team.
3. The Pin is placed on the map at the work location.
4. The manager or owner can add a due date, priority, checklist, followers, or more detail.
5. The assigned user receives the Pin and starts the work.
6. Updates, photos, files, and communication stay inside the Pin.
7. The Pin is completed, leaves active views, and stays searchable for future reference.

## Who gets notified

Department managers may be notified when a Pin is assigned to their department.

Assigned users are notified when a Pin is assigned or reassigned directly to them.

Followers are notified when they are added to a Pin and when activity happens on that Pin.

## Views

**My Pins** is your personal work queue. It shows Pins assigned directly to you.

**Team Pins** shows active work across your department or team.

**Following** shows Pins you created, Pins where you were added as a follower, and Pins you are tracking for visibility.

These views may appear on the Overview dashboard, Pins list, and Map.

## List view and map view

List view is best for sorting through work by title, location, status, assignee, and due date.

Map view is best for seeing where work is happening physically. Pin icons may be color-coded by department or work type, depending on your workspace.

Pins can also appear on equipment records, location pages, and project pages when they are linked.

Groups may appear in the Pins list as folders for similar Pins. Open a group to see the Pins inside it.

## Creating a Pin

Use the create action when you need to report an issue, request work, or flag anything that needs attention.

Required fields usually include a title, assignee, and location. A description, photo, due date, priority, equipment link, checklist, group, tags, or followers can make the Pin easier to complete.

If Snap-to-Pin is available, use the AI sparkle action from the create screen, take a photo, and review the generated title, description, and suggested department before submitting.

Anyone can create a Pin for their own department, another department, or a specific person when their permissions allow it. If a Pin is assigned to a department, managers can route it to the right team member.

## Private Pins

Private Pins are useful for personal tasks, reminders, or sensitive work that should only be visible to the creator and assignee.

If you assign a private Pin to another user, only you and that user can access it. Your workspace may show private Pins with a distinct icon.

## Setting the location

Every Pin should have a location. You may be able to choose a space by searching, use your current position while on property, or inherit a location from linked equipment.

If the location has registered equipment, Pin may ask whether you want to link equipment and optionally update its condition.

## Working inside a Pin

The Info tab is where you take action. Use it to update status, view location, check off checklist items, add attachments, add followers, link equipment, or update details.

Documents and files uploaded to a Pin live on the Pin and are accessible to anyone who can view it. They're also surfaced in the central [Documents module](/modules/documents) for quick access.

The Chat tab is where communication lives. Use it for questions, updates, photos, files, and decisions related to that specific task.

## Followers

Followers have visibility into a Pin and receive updates.

The creator and assignee are usually followers automatically. A user who reassigns a Pin may also become a follower. Additional followers can be added during creation, from the Info tab, or by @ mentioning someone in chat.

## Pin actions

The three-dot menu on a Pin may include actions such as:

- Mark as priority
- Add or adjust a due date
- Edit the title or description
- Link equipment
- Add to a group or project
- Share or export
- Convert to incident

Available actions depend on your permissions.

## Status guide

**To Do** means the Pin is waiting to be started.

**In Progress** means work has started.

**Blocked** means something is preventing completion. Leave a chat message explaining what is blocking the work.

**Cancelled** means the work is no longer needed.

When work is done, mark the Pin complete. Add completion notes, a photo, time spent, and linked-equipment condition updates when your process requires them.
