# Equipment, Inventory, and Locations

Equipment, inventory, and location records help teams connect work to the real assets and spaces they manage.

For deeper module guides, see [Equipment Management](/modules/equipment), [Inventory Management](/modules/inventory), and [Location Management](/modules/locations).

## Equipment

Every piece of equipment can have a digital record with its location, condition, documents, notes, inventory, and linked Pins.

Use Equipment to find assets, review maintenance history, upload manuals or SOPs, check current condition, and create Pins tied to the asset.

## Equipment pages

An equipment page may include:

- Details such as type, manufacturer, model, serial number, location, department, and install date
- Current condition, such as Excellent, Good, Poor, or Out of Service
- Documents and attachments, including manuals, warranties, SOPs, and inspection reports
- Linked parts or inventory
- Active and completed Pins
- Notes about the asset

Documents can be useful for SOPs, manuals, warranties, and inspections. When uploading documents, choose the right visibility for your organization, such as public access or access limited to specific users or departments.

## Equipment condition

Keeping equipment condition current gives managers visibility into what is offline or needs attention.

When equipment is marked Out of Service, it may appear on the Overview dashboard or in reporting for the responsible department.

## Link equipment to a Pin

Link equipment when the work affects a specific asset. This builds service history over time and makes maintenance work easier to reference later.

You can often link equipment while creating a Pin, from an existing Pin, or by creating a Pin directly from the equipment page.

## Inventory

Inventory tracks operational stock, parts, deployable items, and sub-components. Inventory is often managed by department, so your visibility may be limited to the inventory your team is responsible for.

Common inventory uses include:

- Replenishable supplies with stock thresholds
- Spare parts used in maintenance work
- Deployable items that move around the property, with notes for where items were sent
- Sub-equipment components stored as a reference

## Inventory pages

An inventory page may include:

- Item name, category, unit type, cost, supplier, and storage location
- Current stock and minimum or maximum thresholds
- Linked equipment
- Pins linked to restocking or usage
- Notes, including deployment notes

## Low stock alerts

When an inventory item falls below its minimum threshold, Pin may automatically create a restock Pin for the responsible department.

## Locations

Every space can have a location page. Location pages help teams find operational history, documents, equipment, and Pins tied to that space.

Depending on your organization, location pages may be visible across departments so teams can find the operational context for any space.

## Location pages

A location page may include:

- Key details such as building, level, tags, and creation date
- Attachments like photos, PDFs, SOPs, floor plans, or certificates
- Emergency contacts for the location
- Notes
- Active and completed Pins
- Equipment registered at that location

Documents on locations can include SOPs, floor plans, emergency procedures, certificates, or other space-specific files. Use access controls when a document should only be visible to certain users or departments.

## Create work from equipment or locations

Creating a Pin from an equipment page or location page can automatically link the record and inherit the location, reducing manual entry and improving history.
