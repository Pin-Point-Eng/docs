# Manage Pins

Use the Pins section to track work from creation to completion.

## Find Pins

Open **Pins** from the sidebar to view the Pin list. Depending on your workspace, you may be able to filter by status, location, assignee, team, project, due date, or other details.

Open **Map** when you want to find Pins by physical location.

## Pin views

Some workspaces include views at the top of the Pin list or map.

**My Pins** shows Pins assigned directly to you. This is your personal work queue.

**Team Pins** shows active Pins across your department, regardless of who they are assigned to. Managers often use this view to oversee open work.

**Following** shows Pins you created, Pins where you were added as a follower, or Pins you are tracking for visibility. Followers receive updates, messages, and status changes.

## Open a Pin

Select a Pin from the list or map to open its details. The Pin details page can include the description, status, location, assignee, due date, checklist, attachments, related equipment or inventory, chat, and logs.

Inside a Pin, you may see an **Info** tab and a **Chat** tab.

The Info tab holds the details needed to understand and complete the work. Use it to review the Pin, update status, add details, and take action on the task.

The Chat tab keeps conversation tied to that specific Pin. The creator, assignee, managers, and followers can stay in the same conversation.

## Update a Pin

Open the Pin and make the needed change. Common updates include:

- Changing the status
- Updating the assignee
- Editing the due date
- Adding notes
- Uploading photos or files
- Updating checklist items
- Adding related equipment or inventory

Some changes may only be available if you have permission.

## Complete a Pin

When the work is done, open the Pin and update it to the completed status. Add any final notes, photos, time spent, or checklist updates your organization expects.

When a Pin is completed, followers may be notified. The Pin leaves active views, but stays searchable so the work history can be referenced later.

## Delete or restore a Pin

Deleting a Pin may require special permission. If you delete a Pin by mistake and the app offers an undo or restore action, use it right away.

If you cannot delete, restore, or edit a Pin, ask an administrator to confirm your role and permissions.
