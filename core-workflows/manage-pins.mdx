# Manage Pins

Use the Pins section to track work from creation to completion.

![Pin details](/images/pin-details.png)

## Find Pins

Open **Pins** from the sidebar to view the Pin list. Depending on your workspace, you may be able to filter by status, location, assignee, team, project, due date, or other details.

Open **Map** when you want to find Pins by physical location.

## Open a Pin

Select a Pin from the list or map to open its details. The Pin details page can include the description, status, location, assignee, due date, checklist, attachments, related equipment or inventory, chat, and logs.

## Update a Pin

Open the Pin and make the needed change. Common updates include:

- Changing the status
- Updating the assignee
- Editing the due date
- Adding notes
- Uploading photos or files
- Updating checklist items
- Adding related equipment or inventory

Some changes may only be available if you have permission.

## Complete a Pin

When the work is done, open the Pin and update it to the completed status. Add any final notes, photos, or checklist updates your organization expects.

## Delete or restore a Pin

Deleting a Pin may require special permission. If you delete a Pin by mistake and the app offers an undo or restore action, use it right away.

If you cannot delete, restore, or edit a Pin, ask an administrator to confirm your role and permissions.
