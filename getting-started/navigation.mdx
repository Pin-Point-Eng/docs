# Finding your way around

Most Pin work starts from the left sidebar. The exact options you see can depend on your role and permissions.

## Home

Overview gives you a high-level view of current activity.

Notifications shows updates that need your attention.

Chat opens conversations across Pins, teams, and direct messages.

## Operations

Map shows Pins and other work by location.

Pins is where you create, review, update, and complete Pins.

Projects groups related work together.

Equipment stores the assets your team tracks and maintains.

Inventory stores supplies, materials, and stock-related items.

Locations stores the places where work happens.

Incidents is used for incident workflows and reports when your organization uses them.

## Management

Users and Teams are for managing people, departments, and assignments.

Analytics helps managers understand work volume, completion, incidents, equipment, and inventory trends.

Settings contains tenant-level configuration, permissions, roles, request routing, and audit history.

## If you do not see an option

Some sections only appear for users with the right permissions. If a page or button is missing, ask an administrator to confirm your access.
