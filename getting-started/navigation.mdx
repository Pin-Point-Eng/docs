# Finding your way around

Most Pin work starts from the left sidebar. The exact options you see can depend on your role and permissions.

## Home

Overview gives you a personal dashboard for work that needs attention, overdue items, latest notifications, and operational status for your department.

Notifications shows updates that need your attention.

Chat opens conversations across Pins, teams, and direct messages.

## Operations

Map shows active Pins, assets, and incidents at their location across the property.

Pins is where you create, review, update, and complete work. Use the available views to switch between your assigned Pins, department work, and Pins you are following.

Recurring Pins is where scheduled tasks, inspections, patrols, and checklists are managed.

Projects groups multi-department work together with linked Pins, categories, and progress tracking.

Equipment stores digital records for assets, including maintenance history, linked Pins, and relevant documents.

Inventory stores operational stock, including replenishable supplies, spare parts, and deployable assets.

Locations stores the places where work happens, including operational history, documents, and equipment connected to each space.

Incidents is used for incident workflows and reports when your organization uses them.

## Management

Users and Teams are for managing people, departments, and assignments.

Analytics helps managers understand completion rates, response times, workload by team, incidents, equipment, and inventory trends.

Settings contains tenant-level configuration, permissions, roles, request routing, and audit history.

## If you do not see an option

Some sections only appear for users with the right permissions. If a page or button is missing, ask an administrator to confirm your access.
