PIN is available as both a mobile app and a web version at [pin.works](http://pin.works). The mobile app is the primary tool for frontline staff in the field. The web app is used for more detailed management, admin configuration, and settings. 

## Download & Access

- **iOS (App Store):** [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pin-task-management/id6476941513)
- **Android (Google Play):** [Download on the Google Play Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pin-task-management/id6476941513) or Search "PIN — Task Management" in the Google Play Store
- **Web App:** [pin.work](https://pin.works)s

## Logging In

Once your account is created by your admin, you will receive a welcome email with your login credentials and instructions on how to download the PIN app or access the web app at [pin.works](http://pin.works). Follow the steps in the email to activate your account and set your password.

Log in using your email address and the credentials provided in your welcome email. Your department and role have been pre-set by your venue’s admin before your first login.

If you have not received your welcome email or are having trouble accessing your account, contact your manager or the PIN admin team.

## Your Role in PIN

Once you are logged in, what you see in PIN depends on your role. Your role is assigned to you before your first login and determines your level of access across the platform.

PIN has three default roles — Admin, Manager, and User. Your venue may also use additional custom roles depending on how access is configured. Your role will be communicated to you by your manager or the PIN admin team before your first login.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Manager" icon="user-tie">
    Full visibility across your department. Receive all Pins assigned to your department, route and reassign work, set priorities, manage projects, and access analytics. Managers receive notifications for all work created for their department.
  </Card>

  <Card title="User" icon="user">
    Create Pins for any department. Manage and complete assigned work. Communicate within tasks and update equipment status. Receive notifications only for Pins assigned to you or Pins you are a follower on.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="person-military-pointing">
    Full access to everything in PIN — settings, users, teams, all modules, and all data. Responsible for setting up and maintaining the venue’s PIN environment, including managing users, configuring roles and teams, and keeping the system running as operations grow. This role is locked and cannot be modified or deleted.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Roles and access levels are configured per venue by the PIN admin team. Some venues use additional role types such as User — Limited, Location Manager, or Incident Manager. See the [Roles & Permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions) page for full details.
</Info>
