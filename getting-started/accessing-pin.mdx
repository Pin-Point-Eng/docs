PIN is available as both a mobile app and web version at [pin.works](https://pin.works). Both give you full access to everything you can do on the platform. 

## Download & Access

- **iOS (App Store):** [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pin-task-management/id6476941513)
- **Android (Google Play):** Search "PIN — Task Management" in the Google Play Store
- **Web App:** [pin.works](https://pin.works)

## Logging In

Log in using your email address and follow the steps to create your password and activate your account. Your department and role have been pre-set by your venue's admin before your first login.

Your organization code, department, and role will be provided by your manager or the PIN admin team.

If you have not received your login credentials or are having trouble accessing your account, contact your manager or the PIN admin team.

## Your Role in PIN

Once you are logged in, what you see in PIN depends on your role. Your role is assigned to you before your first login and determines your level of access across the platform.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Manager" icon="user-tie">
    Full visibility across your department. Receive all Pins assigned to your department, route and reassign work, set priorities, manage projects, and access analytics. Managers receive notifications for all work created for their department.
  </Card>

  <Card title="User" icon="user">
    Create Pins for any department. Manage and complete assigned work. Communicate within tasks and update equipment status. Receive notifications only for Pins assigned to you or Pins you are a follower on.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Roles and access levels are configured per venue by the PIN admin team. Some venues use additional role types such as User — Limited, Location Manager, or Incident Manager. See the [Roles & Permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions) page for full details.
</Info>
