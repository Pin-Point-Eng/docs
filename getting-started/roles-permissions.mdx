PIN uses a role-based access system. Your role determines what you can see and do across the platform. Roles are configured by your venue's admin team before your first login — the specific roles active at your venue may vary.

<Info>
  The roles listed below represent the full set of available role types in PIN. Your venue may use all of them or a subset. Contact your PIN admin team if you are unsure which role you have been assigned.
</Info>

## Core Roles

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Manager — All Teams Access" icon="user-tie">
    For operational leaders who oversee their own teams but also need visibility into activity across the broader property. Can view Pins, assets, locations, and operational data from other departments to support cross-functional coordination and decision making.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manager — Team Only Access" icon="users">
    For department leaders or in-house third-party vendors who should remain focused on their assigned teams only. Only sees operational activity tied to their assigned teams unless directly added as a follower on a Pin.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="User" icon="user">
    Standard frontline role for day-to-day operational staff. Can create and manage Pins, update statuses, complete tasks, and collaborate within their department's workflows. Notified about Pins assigned to them or Pins they follow, with visibility into all activity within their assigned teams.
  </Card>
  <Card title="User — Limited" icon="user-minus">
    Restricted role for users who should only access work directly relevant to them. Can only view Pins assigned to them, created by them, or Pins they have been added to as followers. No visibility into broader department activity — ideal for vendors, contractors, or temporary staff.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Additional Permission Layers

These roles add specific capabilities on top of a base role and are configured by the admin team.

### Location Manager

Designed for users responsible for overseeing specific physical areas or zones across the property. Location Managers are automatically added as followers on every Pin created within their assigned locations — regardless of which department the Pin belongs to — giving them real-time visibility into all operational activity in their area of responsibility.

Users can configure which locations they want automatic follower access to from their user settings.

### Incident Manager

Designed for users responsible for overseeing active security, safety, and medical incidents across the property. Incident Managers have visibility into all active incidents and associated reports regardless of assignment or follower status, along with full access to historical incident records after closure.

<Warning>
  Only Incident Managers can officially close incidents within the platform. Standard officers and followers can update status and file reports, but cannot close out an incident.
</Warning>

## Custom Roles

If the default roles do not match your venue's operational structure, admins can create custom roles from scratch.

Go to **Settings > Roles & Permissions > Create Role**.

When building a custom role, configure each permission by turning it on or off.

| Permission area | What it controls |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Scope | Whether the user sees all teams, assigned teams, or only their own work. |
| Team Pin Notifications | Whether the user receives notifications for all Pins assigned to their department. |
| Pins | Create and edit Pins, reassign Pins, and delete Pins. |
| Equipment | View, create and edit, or delete equipment. |
| Inventory | View, create and edit, or delete inventory. |
| Incidents | View all incidents, create and edit incidents, and close incidents. |
| Users | Create and edit users or delete users. |
| Departments | Create and edit departments or delete departments. |
| Locations | Edit location details. |
| Analytics | View analytics. |
| Administration | Send broadcasts and manage administrative settings. |

<Tip>
  Start with PIN's default roles if you are unsure. You can always create custom roles later as your operating model becomes clearer.
</Tip>
