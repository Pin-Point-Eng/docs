# What Pin is for

Pin is an operations workspace for location-based work. It helps teams see what needs attention, where it is happening, who owns it, and what changed over time.

## The basic idea

A Pin is a piece of work or an issue that needs attention. A Pin can include:

- A title and description
- A location on the map
- A status
- An assignee or team
- Due dates or recurrence
- Photos, files, or notes
- Related equipment or inventory
- A chat thread
- Activity history

## Who uses Pin

Different people use Pin in different ways.

Workers use Pin to see assigned work, update progress, communicate, and mark tasks complete.

Managers use Pin to assign work, track open items, review team activity, and catch issues before they are missed.

Administrators use Pin to manage users, teams, locations, equipment, inventory, permissions, and settings.

Tenants or external requesters may use a request link or QR code to submit work without needing a full account.

## What Pin helps prevent

Pin is meant to reduce missed tasks, scattered messages, unclear ownership, and work that is hard to find later. Instead of chasing updates across paper forms, text messages, radio calls, or shared drives, teams can keep the work and conversation together.
