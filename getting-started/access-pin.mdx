# Access Pin

Pin is available on the web and as a mobile app. Your organization may provide a direct link, QR code, or app install instructions.

## Web

Open [pin.works](https://pin.works) in your browser and sign in with your email address.

## Mobile

Use the mobile app when you are working around the property and need to create, update, or complete work from the field.

- [iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pin-task-management/id6476941513)
- [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.americandream.pinpoint&pli=1)

## First login

Log in with your email address and follow the steps to create your password and activate your account.

Your organization, department, and role are usually set by your venue administrator. If the information looks wrong, ask an administrator to confirm your account setup.

## Roles

Your role controls what you can see and do in Pin.

Managers usually have visibility across their department, can receive department-wide work, reassign Pins, set priorities, manage projects, and review analytics.

Users usually create Pins, complete assigned work, communicate inside tasks, and update work or equipment status. Users may only receive notifications for Pins assigned to them, Pins they created, or Pins they follow.
