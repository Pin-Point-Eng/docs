---
title: What Is a Pin?
description: A Pin is a digital task — the fundamental unit of work in PIN.
---

A Pin is a digital task. Any issue, request, or job that needs to be done is created as a Pin, assigned to the right person or department, placed on the property map at the exact location where the work takes place, and tracked until it is complete. Every Pin has its own workspace — where all updates, photos, and communication related to that task live together, keeping everyone on the same page from start to finish.

<Info>
  A **Follower** is anyone who has visibility into a Pin and receives notifications when it is updated. The creator and assignee are always added as followers automatically.
</Info>

## The Life of a Pin

| Step | What Happens |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Something needs attention anywhere on the property. |
| 2 | A Pin is created and assigned to the relevant department or individual. |
| 3 | The department manager is notified, can add additional details such as a due date, priority, or checklist, and routes the Pin to a team member. |
| 4 | The assigned user receives the Pin in their feed and starts the work. |
| 5 | The user completes the work, updating the Pin throughout. |
| 6 | The Pin is marked complete and removed from the active map and list views. |

## Who Gets Notified When a Pin Is Created?

- **Department managers** are automatically notified when any Pin is assigned to their department.
- **Assigned users** are notified when a Pin is assigned or re-assigned directly to them.
- **Followers** are notified when they are added to a Pin for visibility.
