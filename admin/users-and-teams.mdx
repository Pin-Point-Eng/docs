# Users and Teams

Administrators use Users and Teams to manage who can access Pin and what work they belong to.

## Users

Use **Users** to add or manage individual people in your organization.

Common user tasks include:

- Adding a user
- Updating a user's name or contact details
- Assigning a role
- Viewing a user's assigned or created Pins
- Removing or disabling access when needed

## Teams

Use **Teams** to group people by department, role, or responsibility.

Teams help route work to the right people. For example, a facilities team may receive maintenance Pins, while a security team may receive incident-related work.

## Roles and permissions

Roles control what a user can see and do. If someone cannot see a section, create an item, edit a Pin, view analytics, or manage settings, their role may not include that permission.

Use **Settings** and **Roles & Permissions** to review or adjust access if your account has permission to do so.

## Best practices

Keep teams aligned with how work is actually assigned.

Give users only the access they need.

Review roles when someone changes responsibilities.

Remove access promptly when a user no longer needs Pin.
