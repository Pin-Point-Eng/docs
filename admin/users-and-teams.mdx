Administrators use Teams and Users to manage who can access PIN and where work should be routed.

Create teams before roles are assigned and before users are added. Every user needs at least one team assignment during setup.

## Teams

Teams represent your venue's departments, such as Maintenance, Housekeeping, Security, Guest Services, Engineering, or Operations.

Teams help route work to the right people. A user's team assignment also affects what they can see, depending on their role.

## Create a team

Go to **Settings > Teams > Create Team**.

Required fields:

- **Name** - the department name as it should appear across PIN
- **Color** - the color used to identify the team's Pins on the property map
- **Icon** - an icon selected from PIN's icon library to visually represent the team

A user can belong to more than one team if their responsibilities span multiple departments.

## Manage teams

Admins can:

- Edit team names and colors
- Add or remove users from teams
- Deactivate teams when they are no longer needed

Historical data tied to a team is retained when the team is deactivated.

## Users

Use **Users** to add or manage individual people in your organization.

Go to **Settings > Users > Create User**.

Required fields:

- **Name** - the user's full name as it should appear across PIN
- **Email address** - used for login and the welcome email
- **Role** - controls the user's access level
- **Team(s)** - the department or departments the user belongs to

Phone number is optional. After account creation, users can add a profile photo from account settings.

## Welcome email

When a user account is created, PIN automatically sends a welcome email with everything they need to get started. The email includes login credentials, instructions to download the PIN mobile app or access the web app at [pin.works](https://pin.works), and steps to activate the account and set a password.

No manual steps are required from the admin side after the account is created.

## Edit a user

Go to **Settings > Users**, select the user, then choose **Edit**.

Admins can:

- Change a user's role when responsibilities change
- Add or remove team assignments
- Update contact information or account details

## Delete or deactivate a user

When someone leaves or no longer needs access, use the user delete action to deactivate their account.

Deleted users can no longer log in, but historical data remains in PIN for reporting and reference. Pins they created, completed work, chat messages, logs, and other operational records are retained.

<Info>
  If the deleted user was assigned to open Pins, those Pins remain in the system and become unassigned. Review unassigned work after removing a user.
</Info>

## You're live

Once your map, teams, roles, and users are set up, your venue is live on PIN. Teams can start creating Pins, working checklists, and managing operations immediately. Organization Settings, Equipment, Recurring Pins, and Inventory can be added whenever your team is ready.

## Roles and permissions

Roles control what a user can see and do. Confirm roles before creating users.

Use **Settings > Roles & Permissions** to review or adjust access if your account has permission to do so.

## Best practices

- Keep teams aligned with how work is actually assigned.
- Give users only the access they need.
- Review roles when someone changes responsibilities.
- Remove access promptly when a user no longer needs PIN.
- After deleting a user, review unassigned Pins and active project followers.
