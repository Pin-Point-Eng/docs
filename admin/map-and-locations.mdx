The map is the foundation of PIN. Every Pin, asset, and incident is placed at its exact physical location on a custom-built digital map of your property.

The map must be built before the rest of setup begins. Without it, Pins and equipment do not have locations to anchor to.

## Building the map

Your venue's map is built by the PIN team using a dedicated mapping CMS that is separate from the PIN platform. Work with your PIN account manager to start the map build early in onboarding.

The map build covers:

- Every floor, zone, and space across the property
- Locations such as retail stores, restaurants, bathrooms, mechanical rooms, parking decks, common areas, and back-of-house spaces
- Multi-floor support so users can navigate between levels

<Warning>
  The map must be finalized and uploaded before you begin other setup steps in PIN. Contact your PIN account manager early if the venue map is not ready.
</Warning>

## Editing locations after upload

Once the map is uploaded, admins can update many location details from the PIN web app.

From a location page, admins can:

- Add or update tags, building, and level information
- Upload SOPs, floor plans, certificates of insurance, emergency procedures, or other files
- Set document access to **Public** (anyone on the platform) or **Specific access** (selected users and/or departments)
- Add emergency contacts such as a store manager name, phone, or email
- Add freeform notes about the space

## What requires the mapping CMS

Some location changes cannot be made from the PIN web app.

Contact your PIN account manager for:

- Renaming a location
- Removing a location
- Structural changes to floors, zones, or mapped areas

For day-to-day location usage, see [Location Management](/modules/locations).
