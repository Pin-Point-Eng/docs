---
title: "Post-Launch Admin Tasks"
description: "Keep access, locations, broadcasts, analytics, and next-step setup current after launch."
---

After your venue is live, admin work shifts from setup to maintenance. Keep access, locations, communications, and reporting current as teams change and operations mature.

## Manage users

Stay current as team members join, move roles, or leave.

- Add new users as team members join
- Update roles or team assignments when responsibilities change
- Deactivate accounts promptly when users leave
- Review unassigned Pins after removing users

## Manage locations

Admins can update many location details from the PIN web app:

- Location tags
- Building and level information
- Emergency contacts
- Documents and access controls — set each document to **Public** or grant **Specific access** to selected users and/or departments
- Notes about the space

For location renames, removals, or structural map changes, contact your PIN account manager.

## Broadcast to users

Use the Broadcast feature to send a platform-wide chat message to all PIN users at your venue.

Broadcasts are useful for:

- Product updates
- Important operational information
- Venue-wide process changes

Only users with the proper role permission can send broadcasts.

## Review analytics

Analytics helps admins monitor how teams are using PIN.

Use Analytics to review:

- Completion rates
- Response times
- Workload by team
- Equipment health
- Inventory levels
- Operational hotspots across the property

Analytics access follows the departments and role scope assigned to the admin.

## What to add after go-live

Once the four required launch steps are done, focus on these next steps in whichever order makes sense for your team.

| Next step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Configure Organization Settings | Set up dispatch calling and broadcast permissions after teams are created. |
| Load equipment | Work with department managers to get key assets into PIN so maintenance history can start building. |
| Set up recurring Pins | Identify critical daily, weekly, and monthly routines and set them up as recurring Pin series. |
| Set up inventory | Load operational stock, especially consumable supplies, spare parts, and deployable assets. |
| Train teams | Share the onboarding guide with managers and frontline staff. |
| Review analytics | After the first few weeks, review how teams are using PIN and refine your setup. |
| Refine roles | Revisit access as teams get comfortable with PIN. |
