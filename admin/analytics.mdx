# Analytics

Analytics helps managers understand what is happening across the organization.

![Analytics](/images/analytics.png)

## What analytics can show

Depending on your workspace setup and permissions, analytics may include:

- Pin volume
- Created versus completed work
- Open or overdue work
- Completion trends
- Incident trends
- Equipment condition or activity
- Inventory activity
- Location hotspots
- Time-based patterns

## Use analytics to answer questions

Examples:

- Which teams have the most open work?
- Are Pins being completed on time?
- Which locations generate the most activity?
- Are incidents increasing or decreasing?
- Is equipment creating repeated work?
- What inventory categories are used most often?

## Permissions

Analytics may be limited to managers or administrators. If you cannot see Analytics, ask an administrator to confirm your access.

## Make analytics more useful

Analytics is only as good as the data entered into Pin. Encourage users to choose accurate locations, statuses, teams, equipment, inventory, and completion details.
