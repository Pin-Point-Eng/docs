As a PIN administrator, you are responsible for setting up and maintaining your venue's PIN environment. That includes configuring the platform before launch, managing users and teams, and keeping the system useful as operations grow.

Getting live on PIN is simple and self-serve. You only need four steps before your teams can start using the platform. Everything else can be added at your own pace after go-live.

## Required go-live steps

PIN requires four setup steps before teams can start using the platform:

1. **Map & Locations** - your venue map must be built and locations mapped before anything else.
2. **Teams** - create your departments with a name, color, and icon.
3. **Roles & Permissions** - use PIN's default roles or create your own.
4. **Users** - create user accounts and assign each user to teams and a role.

Once those four steps are complete, your venue is live and teams can begin using PIN immediately.

<Info>
  Organization Settings, Equipment, Recurring Pins, and Inventory can all be added after go-live at your own pace.
</Info>

## Recommended setup order

| Step | Owner | Required before launch? | Notes |
|------|-------|-------------------------|-------|
| Map build | PIN team and venue admin | Yes | The map is built in PIN's mapping CMS with your account manager. |
| Location review | PIN team and venue admin | Yes | Confirm every floor, zone, and operational space is represented. |
| Teams | Venue admin | Yes | Teams represent departments such as Maintenance, Security, Housekeeping, and Guest Services. |
| Roles | Venue admin | Yes | Use the default Admin, Manager, and User roles or create custom roles. |
| Users | Venue admin | Yes | Users receive a welcome email after account creation. |
| Organization Settings | Venue admin | No | Configure Tenant Portal routing, dispatch calling, and broadcast permissions after teams exist. |
| Equipment | Department managers or implementation team | No | Recommended early so maintenance history can start building. |
| Recurring Pins | Department managers | No | Best after locations and equipment are in place. |
| Inventory | Department managers | No | Add when teams are ready to track stock. |

## Implementation support

Depending on your subscription level, your venue may have an implementation package that covers bulk uploads for equipment, inventory, recurring Pins, and other setup tasks.

Contact your PIN account manager for details on implementation tiers, costs, and what your venue should prepare before launch.
