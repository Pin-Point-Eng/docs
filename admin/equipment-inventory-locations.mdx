# Equipment, Inventory, and Locations

Equipment, Inventory, and Locations help keep work connected to the real-world things and places your team manages.

## Equipment

Use **Equipment** for assets your team maintains, inspects, repairs, or tracks.

Examples may include machines, vehicles, fixtures, devices, or other facility assets.

Equipment records can help teams see work history, related Pins, condition, manufacturer details, or equipment type depending on your workspace setup.

## Inventory

Use **Inventory** for supplies, materials, stock, or parts your team needs to track.

Inventory can help teams connect Pins to the supplies or parts involved in the work.

## Locations

Use **Locations** for the places where work happens.

Locations make Pins easier to find on the map and easier to route to the right team.

## Why these records matter

Good equipment, inventory, and location records make Pins easier to understand and report on later. They also help teams avoid vague work requests and repeated follow-up questions.

## If you cannot edit records

Creating or editing equipment, inventory, suppliers, categories, manufacturers, equipment types, and locations may require special permissions. Ask an administrator if you do not see the option you need.
