# Troubleshooting

## I cannot see a page or button

You may not have permission for that action. Ask an administrator to review your role and access.

## I cannot find a Pin

Try these steps:

1. Check filters in the Pin list.
2. Search by title or other details if search is available.
3. Open the Map and check the expected location.
4. Confirm the Pin has not been completed, deleted, restricted, or assigned to another area.
5. Ask an administrator to confirm your access.

## I cannot create a Pin

Check whether you have permission to create Pins. Also make sure required fields, such as title or location, are filled in.

## I cannot update or complete a Pin

Your role may not allow editing, or the Pin may be restricted. Ask an administrator or the Pin owner for help.

## I am not receiving notifications

Check **Notifications** and **Chat** for unread activity. If expected updates are missing, ask an administrator to confirm that you are assigned, following the Pin, or included in the correct team or chat.

## I added information but analytics did not change

Some analytics may update after data refreshes or after the relevant Pin details are saved. Make sure the Pin has accurate status, location, team, equipment, inventory, and completion information.

## I think something is wrong

Collect the Pin title, location, screenshot, and a short description of what happened. Share that with your administrator or support contact.
