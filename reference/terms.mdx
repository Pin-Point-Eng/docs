# Common terms

## Pin

A trackable item for work, an issue, a task, a request, or something that needs attention.

## Recurring Pin

A Pin that repeats on a schedule, such as a daily check or weekly inspection.

## My Pins

Pins assigned directly to you.

## Team Pins

Active Pins across your department or team.

## Following

Pins you created, were added to, or are tracking for visibility.

## Location

The place where work happens. Locations help teams find work on the map and understand where a Pin belongs.

## Assignee

The person responsible for a Pin.

## Team

A group of users, often based on department or responsibility.

## Status

The current stage of a Pin, such as open, in progress, or completed. Exact status names may vary by workspace.

## Checklist

A list of steps or items that should be completed as part of a Pin.

## Equipment

An asset that can be connected to Pins, such as something your team maintains, inspects, or repairs.

## Inventory

Supplies, parts, materials, or stock that can be tracked and connected to work.

## Chat

Messages related to a Pin, team, group, or person.

## Info tab

The area inside a Pin where the task details, status, assignment, attachments, checklist, and related records live.

## Chat tab

The conversation tied to a specific Pin.

## Notifications

Updates that alert you to activity that may need your attention.

## Permissions

Rules that control what each user can see and do in Pin.

## Documents

Files uploaded into PIN — SOPs, manuals, COIs, floor plans, reports, and any other reference material. Documents can be uploaded to Equipment, Locations, Pins, Incidents, or Projects, and are also accessible through the central Documents module.
