PIN is your venue's single operational platform. Teams use it to log, manage, track, and complete work in one place, tied directly to the physical map of the property.

Every task becomes a Pin dropped on the map at the exact location where the work takes place. That gives teams a shared, real-time view of what is happening, where it is happening, and who is responsible.

## What PIN manages

- Day-to-day tasks and work orders across every department
- Recurring inspections, checklists, and preventive maintenance
- Reusable procedure templates for standard routines and inspections
- Equipment and asset tracking across the entire property
- Inventory and parts management
- Vendors, contractors, and suppliers — profiles with contacts and linked inventory
- Team communication within tasks, between users, and across departments
- Staff scheduling — shift assignments, zone coverage, and break tracking built in
- Multi-department projects and operational initiatives
- Security and safety incidents
- Multi-department projects and initiatives
- Document management — upload SOPs, manuals, COIs, floor plans, and other files anywhere they're relevant, with a central Documents module for access
- Operational analytics

## Available modules

| Module | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Your personal dashboard with Pins, notifications, and items that need attention. |
| Pins | The core workspace where tasks and work orders are created, assigned, tracked, and completed. |
| Map | A live digital map showing active work and assets at their physical locations. |
| Recurring Pins | Automated checklists, inspections, and scheduled tasks that generate on a cadence. |
| Chat | Pin chats, direct messages, group chats, and department conversations. |
| Projects | Coordinated initiatives with linked Pins, categories, progress tracking, and documents. |
| Equipment | Asset records with location, service history, documents, and condition tracking. |
| Inventory | Supplies, parts, deployable assets, and sub-equipment components. |
| Locations | Space-level records with operational history, documents, and related work. |
| Incidents | Security, safety, and medical incidents with timelines and reports. |
| Analytics | Operational performance data, response times, hotspots, and summaries. |
| Documents | A central place to access every document you have permission to view, surfaced from Equipment, Locations, Pins, Incidents, and Projects. |
| Procedures | Reusable checklist templates that can be applied to any Pin or Recurring Pin. Found under Library on web, or More on mobile. |
| Vendors | Profiles for vendors, contractors, and suppliers — with contacts and every linked inventory item in one place. |
| Scheduling | Build department schedules, assign staff to zones, and surface the right person when assigning work. |
