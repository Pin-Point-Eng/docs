# Changelog

What's new in Pin, newest first. Entries tagged **Internal** are engineering, support, and operations notes for the Pin team.

<Update label="October 6, 2026" tags={["Chat","Pinny","Procedures","Pins"]} description="Redesigned chat and a more capable Pinny">
  ### New

  - Added a redesigned chat on web and mobile, laid out like a messaging app: larger avatars, unread badges, two-line previews, and message bubbles with the sender, time, and read ticks inside.
  - Added a simpler way to start a chat: tap a person to open a conversation, or choose **New group** to add members and name it.
  - Added **View changelog** to the **?** Help menu on web, which lists every release and shows a blue dot when there's something new.
  - Added new Pinny actions: it can create recurring series, incidents, projects, and procedures, add notes to equipment, inventory, and locations, add files you send it to a Pin, and attach a procedure to a Pin.
  - Added chats to what Pinny can read: it can search and read chats you're a member of.

  ### Improved

  - Changed Pinny to read everything the app shows you, including incident case numbers and notes, Pin resolution notes, activity logs, vendors, procedures, and documents you can open.
  - Changed Pinny to ask for all missing details in one card with suggested answers, and to ask who a new Pin is assigned to and where it is instead of guessing.
  - Changed Pinny's review cards so that changing a pending request replaces the old card, and to show attached files as thumbnails.
  - Changed Pinny's tables to scroll in their own box with a sticky header, and to open records in the app.
  - Changed mobile chat search to open in place on the Chats tab, with Photos, Videos, and Documents filters and results grouped into Chats and Messages.
  - Changed the archived chats count to stay current after you archive a chat on another device.
  - Changed team email rule pages on web to list every trigger and condition in full.
  - Changed the mobile update prompt back to a full-width banner at the top of the app.

  ### Fixed

  - Fixed two-finger swipe back and forward not working on Mac in the web app.
  - Fixed the chat search bar on iOS only appearing after you pulled the list down.
  - Fixed Pins created from a QR code not being placed on the location's floor.
</Update>

<Update label="October 2, 2026" tags={["Pins","Recurring Pins","Incidents","Analytics"]} description="Meet Pinny, your workspace assistant">
  ### New

  - Added Pinny, a workspace assistant on web: open it from the top bar on any page to ask about your Pins, incidents, and other records, attach photos or PDFs, and have it prepare changes that only apply after you approve them on a review card (turned on per venue).
  - Added Executive Overview on web, which replaces the Overview for roles that have it with a venue health score, its trend, department scores, the areas with the most issues, and incident totals (turned on per venue).
  - Added in-place editing to record pages on web: click a title, description, or detail to change it, with no separate edit dialog.
  - Added a **Create** menu to record pages that starts a related Pin, recurring Pin, incident, or asset with the record already filled in.
  - Added incident reports to the incident page, where they open and edit in place instead of on a separate **Reports** tab.
  - Added an optional due time to recurring series, so each generated Pin is due at a set time instead of the moment it's created.

  ### Improved

  - Changed record page headers to show the next status action and to collapse as you scroll, keeping the tabs in reach.
  - Changed every create page to share one layout, and grouped the **Create** menu into Work and Assets.
  - Improved the media viewer with arrow-key navigation, download, zoom, and PDF previews; it now sizes to the photo, video, or PDF.
  - Improved uploads on web and mobile: each file shows its own progress and can be retried, and on web you can drop files anywhere or paste from the clipboard.
  - Changed forms on web and mobile to ask "Discard changes?" before you leave with unsaved edits.
  - Changed lists to show "Couldn't load" with a way to try again, instead of an empty list, when loading fails.
  - Changed list rows on web to open in a new tab with Cmd-click, Ctrl-click, or a middle click.
  - Changed the mobile app to show when you're offline, and to keep your Pin on screen when creating it fails.
  - Added **Save to Photos** and iOS sharing to the mobile media viewer.

  ### Fixed

  - Fixed "This and all future instances" due date edits being saved on the Pin but not on its recurring series.
  - Fixed creating a one-time Pin on web after backing out of a recurring Pin creating a recurring series instead.
  - Fixed saving an inventory item resetting its stock to 0.
  - Fixed iPhone profile photos not displaying on web.
  - Fixed the web media viewer staying on "Failed to load" after one broken file.
