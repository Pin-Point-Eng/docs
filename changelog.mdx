# Changelog

What's new in Pin, newest first. Entries tagged **Internal** are engineering, support, and operations notes for the Pin team.

<Update label="October 2, 2026" tags={["Pins","Incidents","Media"]} description="Redesigned detail pages">
  ### New

  - Added in-place editing to record pages on web: click a title, description, or detail to change it, with no separate edit dialog.
  - Added a **Create** menu to record pages that starts a related Pin, recurring Pin, incident, or asset with the record already filled in.
  - Added incident reports to the incident page, where they open and edit in place instead of on a separate Reports tab.
  - Added an optional due time to recurring series, so each generated Pin is due at a set time instead of the moment it's created.

  ### Improved

  - Changed record page headers to show the next status action and to collapse as you scroll, keeping the tabs in reach.
  - Changed every create page to share one layout, and grouped the Create menu into Work and Assets.
  - Improved the media viewer with arrow-key navigation, download, zoom, and PDF previews; it now sizes to the photo, video, or PDF.
  - Improved uploads: each file shows its own progress and can be retried, and you can drop files anywhere or paste from the clipboard.
  - Changed forms to ask "Discard changes?" before you leave with unsaved edits.
  - Changed list rows to open in a new tab with Cmd-click, Ctrl-click, or a middle click.
  - Changed the mobile app to show when you're offline, and to keep your Pin on screen when creating it fails.

  ### Fixed

  - Fixed "This and all future instances" due date edits being saved on the Pin but not on its recurring series.
  - Fixed routine rounds such as fire watches being flagged as duplicates of the previous round.
  - Fixed mobile chats keeping their unread badge after you opened them.
  - Fixed pinch to zoom not working on Android.
  - Fixed the Pins tab on Android switching back to its default scope after you picked Department or Following.
  - Fixed saving an inventory item resetting its stock to 0.
  - Fixed cloning a recurring series leaving its attachments behind.
  - Fixed iPhone profile photos not displaying on web.
  - Fixed PDFs on Android opening in Google's viewer instead of your PDF app.
</Update>

<Update label="September 28, 2026" tags={["Pins","Notifications","Map"]} description="Translate any Pin">
  ### New

  - Added [Pin translation](/pins/managing-a-pin#pin-translation): tap **Translate** on any Pin to read its title, description, checklist, form fields, and notes in another language, and **Show original** to switch back.
  - Added an Overdue trigger to team email rules, which sends when a Pin passes its due time without being completed.
  - Added search to the web map, so you can type a location's name and jump to it.

  ### Improved

  - Changed team email rules to also match specific assignees.
  - Changed cloning a Pin to optionally copy its attachments.
  - Changed location previews on the web map to show photos, documents, and tappable emergency-contact phone numbers and emails.
  - Changed each browser tab to stay on its own facility, so you can work in two facilities side by side.
  - Changed the mobile create menu to a new design.
  - Changed Pin PDFs to show times in your venue's timezone.
  - Added a badge for canceled duplicate Pins on web.
  - Changed SSO so it can be required for specific email domains.
</Update>

<Update label="September 3, 2026" tags={["Admin","Notifications"]} description="Team email notifications">
  ### New

  - Added [team email notifications](/admin/team-email-notifications): rules that email anyone about Pin activity, including vendors and contractors without a Pin account. Each rule picks its events, narrows by department, location, equipment, or inventory, and can attach a PDF of the Pin with its photos.
</Update>

<Update label="August 27, 2026" tags={["AI","Pins","Projects","Map","Equipment"]} description="Safety risk and duplicate detection">
  ### New

  - Added safety risk detection: Pin reads a new Pin's text and photo for hazards like exposed wiring or spills, and offers to mark it high priority.
  - Added [duplicate detection](/pins/managing-a-pin#ai-duplicate-pin-detection): a new Pin that matches an open one shows why, and you can keep it or cancel the duplicate.
  - Added cloning a Pin from its **⋯** menu, with every field filled in for you to review.
  - Added comment threads to project categories, and a project log of every change.
  - Added map previews on web: click a location, Pin, or piece of equipment to see its status and active work.
  - Added QR codes for equipment and inventory, printed from web and scanned from the mobile Overview.
  - Added an **Export** button to every list, which downloads a CSV with your filters applied.
  - Added a daily summary email for managers.

  ### Improved

  - Changed AI procedure creation to read PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, CSV files, and images.
  - Added Pin filters for potential duplicates, linked equipment, linked inventory, and attachments.
  - Changed project chats to appear in the Chat module under Projects.
  - Added linking equipment to a vendor.
  - Added Medical and Slip-and-Fall incident filters.
  - Changed Chat's Updates mode to stay on until you turn it off.
  - Added sorting Pins in a group by due date, creation date, or title.
  - Changed the mobile app to warn you before a photo is sent to AI.
</Update>
