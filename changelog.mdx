# Changelog

What's new in Pin, newest first. Entries tagged **Internal** are engineering, support, and operations notes for the Pin team.

<Update label="September 28, 2026" tags={["Pins","Notifications","Map"]} description="Translate any Pin">
  Every Pin can now be read in your own language. Tap **Translate** on a Pin, pick a language, and everything in its details switches over — title, description, checklist, form fields, completion notes, and inventory notes. Tap **Show original** to switch back.

  Anyone who can see a Pin can translate it, on web and mobile. [Learn more](/pins/managing-a-pin#pin-translation)

  ### Overdue alerts in team emails

  [Team email notification rules](/admin/team-email-notifications) can now fire when a Pin passes its due time without being completed — useful for looping in leadership, dispatch, or a vendor when work slips. Rules can also match on specific assignees, alongside department, location, equipment, and inventory.

  ### Improvements

  - Cloning a Pin can now carry its attachments over to the copy
  - Search the web map for any location and jump straight to it
  - Location previews on the web map show photos, documents, and tappable emergency-contact phone numbers and emails
  - Each browser tab on web can stay on a different facility
  - Redesigned create menu on mobile
  - Pin PDFs show times in your venue's timezone
  - Canceled duplicate Pins get their own badge on web
  - Refreshed top bar, notifications, settings, user profiles, and department pages on web
  - SSO can now be required for specific email domains
</Update>

<Update label="September 3, 2026" tags={["Admin","Notifications"]} description="Team email notifications">
  Send automatic emails about Pin activity to anyone — vendors, contractors, tenants, or leadership — without giving them a Pin account.

  Set up rules under **Settings › Team email notifications**. Each rule picks the events that trigger it (created, in progress, blocked, completed, cancelled, or reopened), narrows by department, location, equipment, or inventory, and sends to any To and CC addresses. Rules can attach a PDF of the Pin with its photos.

  Admins can give other roles access with the new **Team email notifications** permission. [Learn more](/admin/team-email-notifications)
</Update>

<Update label="August 27, 2026" tags={["AI","Pins","Projects","Map","Equipment","Vendors"]} description="Safety risk and duplicate detection">
  Pin's AI now looks out for two things whenever someone creates a Pin.

  **Safety risks.** Pin reads the title, description, and any Snap-to-Pin photo for urgent hazards like exposed wiring or spills. If it finds one, a **Safety Risk Detected** banner lets you mark the Pin high priority in one tap — or dismiss it. Nothing changes unless you choose to.

  **Duplicates.** Each new Pin is checked against existing open Pins. If it looks like a repeat, the Info tab explains the match and how far away the other Pin is, and you can keep it or cancel the duplicate. [Learn more](/pins/managing-a-pin#ai-duplicate-pin-detection)

  ### Clone any Pin

  Copy a Pin from its **⋯** menu, including its assignee, checklist, followers, linked equipment and inventory, and attachments. You review everything before it's created. [Learn more](/pins/managing-a-pin#cloning-a-pin)

  ### Projects

  Every project category now has its own comment thread, and you can pick the category while creating a Pin. A project log records every change, and project chats appear under the **Projects** tab in [Chat](/communication/chat). [Learn more](/modules/projects)

  ### Map previews on web

  Click a location, Pin, or piece of equipment on the [map](/viewing-navigating/map-view) to see its status, active work, and emergency contacts without leaving the map.

  ### QR codes and CSV exports

  Print QR codes for equipment and inventory from the web, then scan them from the mobile Overview to jump straight to the record. Every list page now has an **Export** button that respects your filters.

  ### Improvements

  - AI procedure creation accepts PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, CSV files, and images
  - New Pin filters for potential duplicates, linked equipment, linked inventory, and attachments
  - Link each piece of equipment to a vendor
  - Managers get a daily email summarizing yesterday's activity
  - Medical and Slip-and-Fall filters for incidents
  - Chat's Updates mode stays on until you turn it off
  - Sort Pins in a group by due date, creation date, or title
  - Search everything from one place on mobile
  - Photos and mentions in project chats
  - Mobile warns you before a photo is sent to AI
  - Recurring Pins keep checklist sections when cloned
</Update>

<Update label="July 10, 2026" tags={["AI","Procedures","Recurring Pins"]} description="Build checklists with AI">
  Turn any checklist into a [procedure](/modules/procedures) or recurring Pin checklist in seconds. Tap ✨ on the create screen, then take a photo of a printed sheet, upload a file, or describe what you need. Pin drafts the items for you to review and save.

  Available on web and mobile.
</Update>

<Update label="July 8, 2026" tags={["Locations"]} description="Location status">
  Every [location](/modules/locations) now shows whether it's ready to use. Tap the status at the top of a location to set it to **Operational**, **Occupied**, **Needs Attention**, **Out of Service / Closed**, or **Vacant**.

  Anyone with access to a location can change its status, and you can filter the Locations list by status to see every room in a given state.
</Update>

<Update label="July 3, 2026" tags={["Vendors","Inventory"]} description="Vendors">
  Keep every vendor, contractor, and supplier in one place. Each [vendor](/modules/vendors) has a profile with its contacts, the inventory it supplies, and documents like contracts, W-9s, and certificates of insurance.

  Inventory items now link to a real vendor instead of a free-text supplier. Vendors belong to departments, so teams see the vendors they work with; admins see them all.

  Find **Vendors** in the sidebar.
</Update>

<Update label="June 30, 2026" tags={["Procedures","Checklists"]} description="Procedure templates">
  Save a checklist once and reuse it anywhere. The new **Procedures** library holds your standard checklists — opening walk-throughs, inspections, turnovers — and anyone can add one to a Pin or recurring Pin with **Use template**.

  Long checklists can now be split into collapsible sections. Editing a template only affects Pins created afterward. [Learn more](/modules/procedures)
</Update>

<Update label="June 12, 2026" tags={["Documents"]} description="Documents">
  Attach documents wherever the work happens — equipment, locations, Pins, incidents, and projects — and find all of them in the new **Documents** module.

  Documents on Pins, incidents, and projects are visible to that record's followers. On equipment and locations, you choose whether a document is public or shared with specific people and departments.
</Update>

<Update label="June 3, 2026" tags={["Tenant Portal","Locations"]} description="QR codes that route to the right team">
  Each request QR code now sends submissions straight to the team that should handle them. Generate a code from the Overview or any [location](/modules/locations), pick the team — Front Desk, Housekeeping, and so on — and optionally lock it to that location.

  People submitting requests can see which team their report is going to, and can opt in to email updates as the Pin moves to completion. [Learn more](/modules/tenant-portal)

  ### Improvements

  - QR links are signed, so outdated or altered links ask the person to scan a fresh code
  - Routing is chosen per code, replacing the organization-wide default team setting
</Update>

<Update label="June 2, 2026" tags={["Notifications","Pins"]} description="Notifications follow your account">
  Switching accounts on the same phone now moves push [notifications](/onboarding/chat-and-notifications) with you, so you no longer get alerts for an account you've signed out of.

  ### Improvements

  - Hardened how web and mobile handle network requests and file uploads

  ### Fixes

  - The group count on the mobile Pins tab now respects your department filters
  - Uploads no longer fail in browsers that report progress incorrectly
</Update>

<Update label="June 1, 2026" tags={["Projects","Permissions","Pins"]} description="Project permissions">
  Projects now have their own permissions, the same way incidents do. **View All** lets someone see every project, not just the ones they follow, and **Create**, **Edit**, and **Delete** control the matching actions.

  Admins have all four automatically. Grant them to other roles under **Settings › Roles & Permissions**. [Learn more](/getting-started/roles-permissions)

  ### Improvements

  - Remove a follower right from its chip on Pins and incidents
  - The followers picker opens departments that already have followers, counts selections, and searches across every department
  - Pin groups only show groups with Pins you can see
  - Every delete and confirm prompt on web looks and behaves the same, and an accidental click outside no longer closes it
</Update>

<Update label="May 27, 2026" tags={["Authentication","Locations","Recurring Pins"]} description="Single sign-on">

  ### Managed buildings

  ### Improvements

  - Choose whether a recurring Pin's title or description change applies to one Pin or future ones too
  - Remove a due date from a Pin on web

  - "Resolve" and "Resolved" are used consistently across web and mobile
  - A better date-range picker and creation-platform chart in analytics
</Update>

<Update label="May 27, 2026" tags={["Internal","Database","Deployment"]} description="Engineering notes">
  - Postgres row-level security is enforced, with a read-only `yarn db:check-rls` command and automated checks for tenant models
  - Deploy routing for `main` and `staging` moved into the shared deploy workflow
  - WorkOS SSO adds server routes, settings, bypass handling, audit events, and migrations. The app still boots without SSO environment variables, but SSO flows report service-unavailable until configured.
  - The building migration creates managed Building rows, links locations, and drops the old free-text column. Spot-check location counts for large venues before editing buildings.
  - Write permissions split into separate create and edit permissions, with old stored permissions converted
  - Removing a mobile attachment disconnects the media instead of soft-deleting it first, avoiding tenant-scope validation failures. Orphaned media cleanup is still open.
</Update>
