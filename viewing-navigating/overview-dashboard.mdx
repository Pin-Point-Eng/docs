The Overview is the first screen you see when you open PIN — a live summary of everything that needs your attention right now. PIN gives you several ways to find and view your work, from this personal dashboard to a full map of the property.

## Overview Cards

The cards at the top of your Overview show your three main views, available throughout the platform:

| Card | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| My Pins | Pins currently assigned directly to you. |
| Following | All Pins you are following — Pins you created or were added to as a follower. |
| Team Pins | All active Pins across your department. |
| Incidents | Active security and safety incidents that you are following. |

## Below the Cards

- **Overdue & Due Soon** — Pins you are associated with that are past their due date or coming up soon.
- **Latest Notifications** — Recent activity on Pins you are involved in. Tap any item to go directly to that Pin.
- **Equipment Offline** — All equipment within your department that has its status set to Out of Service.
- **Projects** — Active projects you are a follower on, with quick access to linked Pins, progress, and project-level chat.
- **Analytics** — Managers have access to a dedicated Analytics section showing completion rates, response times, workload by team, location hotspots, and more.

## Notifications

PIN sends you real-time notifications for:

- A Pin being assigned to you
- A new message in a Pin chat you are part of
- A status update on a Pin you are following
- A Pin being completed that you are following
- Being added as a follower to a Pin

Tap any notification to jump directly to the Pin it relates to.

<Info>
  **Do Not Disturb:** If you are off for the day and do not want to receive PIN notifications, you can enable Do Not Disturb in your profile settings. This will pause all notifications until you turn it off.
</Info>

## Daily Summary Email (Managers)

Every manager receives a daily email summarizing the previous day's operational activity across their department and property.

The email includes:

- **Pins Completed** — total, broken down by recurring vs. non-recurring
- **Pins Created** — total, broken down by recurring vs. non-recurring
- **Active Members** — count of team members active during the day
- **Time Logged** — total time logged across all Pins
- **Completed breakdown** — recurring completion percentage plus a list of non-recurring completions
- **Department Activity** — individual user completions and time logged

An **Open in PIN** button in the email takes you directly into the platform. Users can unsubscribe from the email at any time from their profile settings.

## QR Code Scanner (Mobile)

The mobile app's Overview screen has a **QR code scanner** button at the top. Tap it to open the camera and scan any equipment or inventory QR code — the scanned code opens the linked record's detail page directly.

See [Equipment](/modules/equipment#qr-codes-for-equipment) and [Inventory](/modules/inventory#qr-codes-for-inventory) for details on generating printable QR codes.
