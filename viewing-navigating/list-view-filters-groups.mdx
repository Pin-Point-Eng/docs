List view gives you a scrollable, structured view of all your Pins in chronological order with their key details — title, location, status, assignee, and more. This is the easiest place to manage and work through your Pins.

  ![IMG 1360 Portrait](/images/IMG_1360-portrait.png)
</Frame>

## Scope Views

At the top of both List and Map views, toggle between three scopes:

| Scope | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Team | All active Pins across your department — the full picture of what your team is working on. (Managers and Users only. Field Users do not see this view.) |
| My Pins | Only the Pins assigned directly to you. |
| Following | All Pins you are following — because you created them, were added as a follower, or added yourself. |

## Within Your List

- **Recurring Pins** — identified by the recurring arrows icon next to the title. Tapping a recurring Pin opens that specific instance.
- **Groups** — folders that organize similar Pins together. Groups appear inline in your list. Tap a group to see all the Pins inside it.

## Filters & Search

Use the filter menu to narrow your view by status, priority, date range, recurring frequency, or groups. In List View, the search bar at the top lets you quickly find any Pin by title.

<Frame>
  ![IMG 2369 Portrait](/images/IMG_2369-portrait.png)
</Frame>

## Other Places to View Pins

Pins can also be found in other parts of the platform:

- **Equipment pages** — any Pin linked to a piece of equipment can be viewed from that equipment's record, including active and completed history.
- **Location pages** — any Pin created at a specific location can be viewed from that location's page.
- **Projects** — if you are a follower on a project, you can view all Pins linked to that project from within the Projects module.
