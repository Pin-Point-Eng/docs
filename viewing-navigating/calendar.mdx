The Calendar is a time-based view of your scheduled work across the property. It sits alongside Overview, Map, and List View in the sidebar and gives managers and teams a single place to see what's due when.

Available on the **web app only**.

## What appears on the Calendar

The Calendar surfaces anything with a date attached:

- **Scheduled Pins** — Pins with a due date, placed on the day they're due
- **Recurring Pin occurrences** — upcoming instances across the date range
- **Project target dates** — shown on the day each project is due

Each item is **colored by department** so you can tell at a glance which teams own the day. Multi-day items (like a weekly inspection series) stretch across the days they span.

## Views

Toggle between three views at the top of the Calendar:

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Month** | A full calendar grid for the month, with every scheduled item placed on its day. Default view. |
| **Week** | Seven-day view with more room per day for busy teams. |
| **Day** | Single-day view for detailed planning. |

Use the **prev / next arrows** at the top right to move through time, or tap **Today** to jump back to the current date.

When a day has more items than fit on screen, a **+N more** indicator shows how many are hidden — click it to expand that day.

## Filters

The Calendar has the same filter options as List View, so you can scope what you see:

- **Department** — narrow to one or more departments using the Department picker at the top right
- **Filter panel** — click **Filter** to open the full filter set, including status, priority, assignee, linked equipment and inventory, and more

Filters stack — combine them to focus on exactly what you need.

## Rescheduling by Drag

To reschedule a Pin, **drag its card to a new day** on the Calendar. The Pin's due date updates immediately. For multi-day items, drag either end to extend or shorten the span, or drag the body of the card to move the entire range.

Drag-to-reschedule respects your role's permissions — if your role can't edit a Pin, you can't reschedule it from the Calendar either.

## Opening an Item

Click any Pin, recurring occurrence, or project target on the Calendar to open its full detail page. From there you can update the status, chat with followers, or edit any detail as usual.
