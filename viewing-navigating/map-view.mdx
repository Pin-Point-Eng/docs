The map is the foundation of PIN. Every Pin and asset is tied to a precise location on your venue's custom digital map — so instead of tasks living in disconnected lists, everything is anchored to where the work actually happens.

Access the map from the Pins section on mobile by tapping the Map tab at the top, or from Map in the left nav on the web app.

## Navigating the Map

- **Pin icons** are color-coded to match the department they are assigned to — making it easy to see at a glance which teams are active and where.
- **Scope toggles** (Team, My Pins, Following) apply to the map, so what you see is always filtered to your current view.
- **Tap any Pin** on the map to open its full detail screen.
- **Filters** let you narrow what you see by team, status, priority, or equipment type.
- The map view is also available across other modules — including Equipment, Inventory, incidents, and Locations — each showing their own layer across the property.
