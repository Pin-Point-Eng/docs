PIN gives you several ways to find, view, and move through your work — whether you want a personal summary of what needs attention, a live picture of activity across the property, or a structured list you can filter and group.

This section walks through the three core views you'll use every day, and how each one helps you stay oriented.

## How the views work together

- **Overview** is the fastest way to see what's on your plate today.
- **Map View** is the fastest way to understand _where_ work is happening.
- **List View** is the fastest way to dig into a specific slice of work — by status, priority, location, assignee, or category.

You can switch between views at any time. Pins, filters, and context follow you across the platform, so you always stay grounded in the same operational picture.
