The Chat module replaces scattered radio calls, text threads, and emails with one centralized place to communicate — both with teammates directly and around specific operational tasks. Every conversation is searchable and stored permanently as part of the operational record.

All chats can be found within the **Chat module**, giving you one place to see and manage all conversations across the platform.

## Types of Chat

| Type | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pin Chat** | Built into every Pin. Automatically includes the creator, assignee, all followers, and any managers within the assigned department. Where most operational communication happens. |
| **Direct Messages** | One-on-one messaging with any user on the platform. |
| **Group Chats** | Create a conversation with multiple users across teams or departments. |
| **Team Chat** | Automatically includes every member of your department — useful for broadcasts and team-wide coordination. |

## Pin Chat

Pin Chat is the most important communication tool in PIN. Because every Pin has its own chat — automatically including the creator, assignee, followers, and assigned-department managers — context lives with the task instead of scattered across DMs and radio calls.

Every new message triggers a real-time notification for everyone in that chat, and the full thread stays attached to the Pin permanently as part of the operational record.

- **@ mention** any user to instantly add them as a follower and notify them — pulls someone into the Pin without manually adding them
- **Share photos, videos, and documents** directly within the chat
- **View the activity log** — every status change, assignee update, follower addition, and completion event in one chronological view alongside the conversation

## Messages vs. Updates

Within any chat, you have two ways to communicate:

| Type | When to Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Regular Message** | Back-and-forth conversation, questions, quick notes. |
| **Update** | Important developments everyone needs to see — status changes, escalations, significant findings. Highlighted in **green** so they stand out in the thread. |

<Tip>
  Use Updates for anything you want all followers to clearly notice — they stand out visually and are easy to find when reviewing a Pin's history later.
</Tip>

## Finding & Searching Chats

- Open the **Chat module** to see all your active conversations
- Open any **Pin** to access its built-in chat
- Search any conversation by keyword, person, or date
- Department members all see the **Team Chat** by default
